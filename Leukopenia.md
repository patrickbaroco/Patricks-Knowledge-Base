Leukopenia as an underweighted early clue to systemic autoimmune disease, particularly lupus.

## How it operates

- **Never let leukopenia in a young woman with a new inflammatory syndrome pass unremarked.** It is a powerful but easily overlooked signal toward systemic lupus erythematosus, well before classic features (malar rash, oral ulcers, alopecia) are appreciated.
- **It can predate the recognizable disease.** Leukopenia may be the earliest lab clue available, arriving before the clinical picture looks anything like a textbook presentation of the underlying autoimmune disease.

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **The clue hiding behind a mechanistic debate.** While the team spends significant time on the mechanics of angioedema (histamine versus bradykinin, complement pathway interpretation), a mild, easily-dismissed leukopenia is the thread that — combined with low complements — ultimately points to systemic lupus erythematosus as the true diagnosis.

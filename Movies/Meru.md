Three elite climbers battle obsession, personal loss, and the mountain itself during a grueling attempt on the Shark's Fin route of Mount Meru in the Himalayas.

Directed by [[Jimmy Chin]] and [[Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi]], who also made [[Free Solo]] and [[The Rescue]]. Often paired with [[The Alpinist]] and [[14 Peaks - Nothing Is Impossible]] as essential extreme-climbing documentaries.

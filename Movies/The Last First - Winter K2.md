During the crowded 2021 race for the first winter ascent of K2, Icelander John Snorri Sigurjónsson and Pakistani father and son Ali and Sajid Sadpara push for the summit alongside commercial clients, influencer climbers, and a Nepali team determined to claim the prize for themselves. Amir Bar-Lev's film becomes an unflinching look at the money, class, and ambition reshaping high-altitude mountaineering, and at the tragedy that followed.

Often paired with [[14 Peaks - Nothing Is Impossible]] (Nimsdai Purja's Nepali team made the winter summit this film covers), [[Meru]], [[The Alpinist]], and [[Free Solo]] as essential extreme-climbing documentaries.

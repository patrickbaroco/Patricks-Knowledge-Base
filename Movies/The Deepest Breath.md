A world-record-chasing freediver and her safety diver form a deep bond while pursuing an extreme underwater goal, in a documentary that captures both the beauty and the danger of freediving to record depths.

Often thought of alongside [[Diving into the Unknown]] and [[The Rescue]] as underwater-risk documentaries.

Rock climber Alex Honnold pursues his dream of scaling Yosemite's 3,000-foot El Capitan with no ropes or safety gear, in what becomes the most daring free solo ascent ever recorded.

Directed by [[Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi]] and [[Jimmy Chin]], who also made [[The Rescue]] and [[Meru]]. Often paired with [[The Alpinist]] and [[14 Peaks - Nothing Is Impossible]] as essential extreme-climbing documentaries.

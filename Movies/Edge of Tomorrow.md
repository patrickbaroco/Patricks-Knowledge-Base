A public-relations officer with no combat experience is dropped into a losing war against an alien invasion, and finds himself trapped in a time loop that resets to the same morning every time he dies. Each cycle he gets a little better at fighting — and a little closer to the soldier who might know how to end it.

Often thought of alongside [[Source Code]] and [[Groundhog Day]] as a time-loop story.

When an oxygen tank explodes on the way to the moon in 1970, astronauts Jim Lovell, Fred Haise, and Jack Swigert and the ground team at Mission Control improvise a way to bring the crippled spacecraft home.

Often thought of alongside [[The Martian]] as a problem-solving survival story in space.

Built almost entirely from archival footage, this documentary follows Brazilian driver Ayrton Senna from his Formula One debut through three world championships to his death at Imola at age 34.

Often thought of alongside [[Rush]] and [[Ford v Ferrari]] as racing stories of rivalry and risk.

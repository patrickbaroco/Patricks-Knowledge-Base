After a painful breakup, a man discovers his ex-girlfriend has had all memory of their relationship erased and undergoes the same procedure — then fights from inside his own collapsing memories to hold onto her.

Written by [[Charlie Kaufman]]. Often thought of alongside [[Her]] as a bittersweet sci-fi romance about love mediated by technology.

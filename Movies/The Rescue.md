Using exclusive interviews and never-before-seen footage, this documentary chronicles the 2018 rescue of twelve boys and their soccer coach from a flooded cave in northern Thailand, one of the most perilous rescue operations in modern history.

Directed by [[Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi]] and [[Jimmy Chin]], who also made [[Free Solo]] and [[Meru]]. Also often paired with [[Diving into the Unknown]] as a cave-diving story.

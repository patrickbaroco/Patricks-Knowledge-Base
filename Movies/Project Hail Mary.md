Science teacher Ryland Grace wakes alone aboard a spacecraft with no memory of how he got there, and gradually realizes he is humanity's last chance to stop a microorganism that is dimming the sun. An unexpected companion changes the nature of his solitary mission.

Often thought of alongside [[The Martian]] as Andy Weir's problem-solving survival stories in space.

An insurance salesman gradually realizes his entire hometown is a television set and every person in his life an actor, in a round-the-clock broadcast that has aired since his birth.

Often thought of alongside [[Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind]] as a high-concept Jim Carrey film with real emotional weight.

Marc-André Leclerc, a young Canadian climber, makes daring solo ascents without ropes or safety gear on remote peaks; filmmakers document his elusive life and a historic climb in Patagonia.

Often paired with [[Free Solo]], [[Meru]], and [[14 Peaks - Nothing Is Impossible]] as essential extreme-climbing documentaries.

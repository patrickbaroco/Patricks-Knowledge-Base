CIA officer Carrie Mathison, working while hiding a bipolar diagnosis, becomes convinced that a rescued Marine POW has been turned by al-Qaeda, and the series follows her through a decade of counterterrorism work at home and abroad.

Watched as a family with the boys; Season 1 was the rough one content-wise but manageable. Fizzled out after Season 2 or 3 and never finished the run. Often thought of alongside [[Slow Horses]] as a character-driven spy series.

Anthology crime series; each season follows a different police investigation. Season 1 pairs Louisiana detectives Rust Cohle and Marty Hart across seventeen years as they pursue a ritualistic killer and are undone by their own philosophies and failures.

Season 1 is a personal favorite. Often thought of alongside [[Mare of Easttown]], [[Sharp Objects]], and [[Fargo]] as atmospheric prestige crime television.

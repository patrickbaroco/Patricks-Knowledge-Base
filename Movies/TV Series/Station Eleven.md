Twenty years after a flu pandemic collapses civilization, a traveling Shakespeare troupe crosses the Great Lakes region as the story braids survivors' lives back to the night the world ended. Adapted from Emily St. John Mandel's novel.

Showrunner Patrick Somerville wrote on [[The Leftovers]]; the two are often thought of together as post-catastrophe stories about grief and meaning rather than survival.

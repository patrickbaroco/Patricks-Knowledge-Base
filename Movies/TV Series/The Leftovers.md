Three years after two percent of the world's population vanishes without explanation, police chief Kevin Garvey and the town of Mapleton try to go on living with an event no one can explain. Seasons 2 and 3 are widely regarded as the peak.

Often thought of alongside [[Station Eleven]] and [[Pluribus]] as inexplicable-event dramas about grief and meaning.

After a flu pandemic wipes out most of humanity, a former pilot and a hardened ex-Marine guard their refuge while holding onto hope for a better life beyond it.

Based on Peter Heller's novel, which Patrick read and loved. Critics were cool on the adaptation (41% on Rotten Tomatoes, 70% audience score), faulting its slow pacing and thin characters while praising the visuals and the performances of Jacob Elordi and Josh Brolin.

Often thought of alongside [[Station Eleven]] and [[Children of Men]] as post-pandemic stories more interested in what makes life worth living than in survival alone.

A team of Finnish and Norwegian cave divers loses two of their own during a dive in Norway's treacherous Plura cave system. When authorities deem recovery too dangerous and call off the search, the surviving divers mount their own perilous, self-filmed mission to bring their friends' bodies home.

Often paired with [[The Rescue]] and [[The Deepest Breath]] as underwater-risk documentaries.

# Clinical Governance: Two Paths to Launch

*The same two committees, authorized two ways*

---

Both paths produce the same structure. The Clinical Quality Committee sets the standards of care, teaches them, and audits against them. The Peer Review Committee reviews the care of individual clinicians and decides, on Eventus's behalf, matters concerning an individual clinician's competence or professional conduct. There is no third committee. The Chair of Clinical Excellence chairs both, presides over the Peer Review Committee without a vote, and signs its decisions. A clinician who asks for a hearing is heard by clinicians who took no part in the review. The Chair reports quarterly to the CMO and COO.

The paths differ in who confers that authority, and in what follows from it.

| | Path A: Board delegation | Path B: CEO authorization |
|---|---|---|
| Who creates the committees | The board, by a resolution the CEO brings forward | The CEO, by signing a one-page authorization |
| Who can withdraw the Peer Review Committee's authority | The board | The CEO |
| Who approves later changes to the charters | The board | The CEO |
| The hearing procedure | A separate Fair Hearing Procedure, adopted with the charters | Written into the Peer Review Committee's charter |
| Reporting beyond the quarterly meeting | An annual aggregate summary to the board through the CMO, and prompt notice to the board of four events | The CEO receives the quarterly summary and prompt notice of the same four events |
| What gets signed | Resolution, two charters, Fair Hearing Procedure | Authorization, two charters |

Which path is open depends on who, under Eventus's governing documents, can create a committee that acts as the company itself. Ben can confirm that, and counsel will confirm it against the privilege statutes in our five states. Indiana's statute protects committees organized by the governing body, so Indiana is the state where Path B may need more than the CEO's signature.

Each path has two documents: a description of how the structure works (A1, B1), and the instrument that launches it (A2, B2).

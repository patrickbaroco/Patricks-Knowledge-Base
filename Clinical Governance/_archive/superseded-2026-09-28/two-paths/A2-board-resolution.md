# Action of the Board of Directors by Written Consent

*Establishment of the Clinical Quality Committee and the Peer Review Committee*

---

The undersigned, being all of the directors of [legal name of entity] ("Eventus"), adopt the following resolutions by written consent without a meeting.

**WHEREAS**, Eventus provides care through physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants and other licensed clinicians in long-term care and assisted living settings in Virginia, North Carolina, Ohio, Indiana and Kentucky; and

**WHEREAS**, the Board is responsible for oversight of the quality of care Eventus provides, and has determined that Eventus should set its clinical standards, and review the care furnished by individual clinicians, through a formal process conducted by clinical peers for the purpose of furthering quality health care;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:**

## 1. Establishment

**RESOLVED**, that the Clinical Quality Committee and the Peer Review Committee are established as committees of Eventus, and that the charters attached as Exhibits A and B are adopted.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each committee is established as a peer review, quality assurance and professional review committee of Eventus within the meaning of 42 U.S.C. § 11101 et seq., Va. Code § 8.01-581.17, Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.25 et seq., N.C. Gen. Stat. § 90-21.22A, Ind. Code § 34-30-15, and Ky. Rev. Stat. § 311.377.

## 2. Authority of the Peer Review Committee

**RESOLVED**, that the Board delegates to the Peer Review Committee the authority to decide, on behalf of Eventus, matters concerning the professional competence or conduct of an individual clinician, including any action affecting what that clinician may do, including restriction, and what is reported to the National Practitioner Data Bank and to state licensing boards. A decision of the Committee in those matters, signed by the Chair of Clinical Excellence, is the action of Eventus. The Committee has no authority over staffing, employment, operations or company policy.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board takes no part in individual cases and hears no appeal from any decision of the Committee, and that the Board may withdraw this delegation at any time.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no person who holds authority over the employment, compensation or assignment of clinicians shall sit on the Peer Review Committee, review a case, or direct any decision of the Committee.

## 3. Chair and Members

**RESOLVED**, that the Chair of Clinical Excellence chairs both committees and appoints their members, and presides over the Peer Review Committee without a vote.

## 4. Fair Hearing and Health Matters

**RESOLVED**, that the Fair Hearing Procedure attached as Exhibit C is adopted, under which a clinician may request a hearing before a panel of clinicians who took no part in the review before any restriction takes effect.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that Human Resources is designated as the pathway for questions of a clinician's health, impairment or fitness to practice.

## 5. Protection of Participants

**RESOLVED**, that Eventus shall defend and indemnify each committee member, hearing panel member, external reviewer, and each person assisting any of them, against claims, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from their good-faith service, to the fullest extent permitted by law, and shall advance expenses as incurred; and that the officers of Eventus shall confirm and maintain insurance coverage for that service.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that no person shall suffer an adverse employment action by reason of good-faith participation in the work of either committee.

## 6. Reporting

**RESOLVED**, that the Chair of Clinical Excellence shall meet quarterly with the Chief Medical Officer and the Chief Operating Officer and report in aggregate on the work of both committees; that management shall receive notice of any final action affecting a clinician's practice, stating the action, when it takes effect and how long it lasts; that the Board shall receive, through the Chief Medical Officer, an annual summary in the same aggregate form; and that the Board shall be notified promptly of any litigation arising from a review, any report to the National Practitioner Data Bank, any restriction lasting more than thirty days, and any allegation that the required process was not followed. No report under this section shall identify a clinician or describe a case, except as a notice of one of the four listed events requires.

## 7. Reserved to the Board, and Further Acts

**RESOLVED**, that any change to the charters or to the Fair Hearing Procedure requires the approval of the Board; and that the officers of Eventus are authorized and directed to register Eventus with the National Practitioner Data Bank as a reporting entity and to take all other action necessary to carry these resolutions into effect, which take effect on [date].

# PRC Charter simplification, 2026-11-16

**Result.** The operative text (Articles I to XVI) falls from about 21,650 words to about 18,160, roughly a sixth. Article IX, the pilot, fell by a third; the other articles are denser in rules and gave up less. No substantive decision authority, practitioner right, restriction limit, voting threshold, or external reporting duty changes. The day-20 reportability reminder moves from the Charter to a binding operating procedure, whose text is in Part C and takes effect with the shortened Charter; OP-1 states its scope and adds a written notice and a register entry the Charter sentence did not have. One recording duty is consolidated: the record of the circumstances of a resignation, formerly in §§ 9.6 and 10.9, now sits in § 15.9 (Part E). One heading is corrected (§ 9.5), and three passages are cut rather than moved because they made claims the Charter should not carry (§§ 7.2, 7.3, 9.8(g)).

**Method.** Each paragraph was sorted as a rule, a reason, a restatement, or an administrative instruction. Rules stay. Reasons go to the member guide, in its new section *Why the Charter says what it says*. Text is deleted where speculative, legally inaccurate, duplicative, or unnecessary to explain the rule. Restatements are cut where another section governs. Notices were kept wherever they serve committee oversight or patient safety, whether or not missing one would favor the Practitioner. Deadlines protecting Practitioners, automatic expiry, voting rules and conditions for extending a restriction stay in the Charter.

**Not changed by this pass.** The Open items section, the bracketed defaults, and every rule not listed below. Pre-simplification copy: `_archive/prc-charter-draft-2026-11-16-before-simplification.md`.

## C. Operating procedure: text moved from the Charter

Adopted with the shortened Charter, under the approval in § 16.2, and effective on the same date. The shortened Charter is not adopted without it; otherwise the reminder would have been removed rather than moved.

**OP-1. Thirty-day reportability prompt.** For each restriction under § 9.1 of the Charter that remains in force on the [twentieth] day after imposition, the Coordinator gives the Chair and the members written notice on that day, stating the date on which the restriction will have been in effect for thirty days under § 9.4, so that the Committee can determine reportability with counsel under § 15.7 before that date. Where a restriction under § 9.1 follows one under § 9.8 in the same matter, the day is counted from the first imposition, as § 9.8(f) provides; where that day has already passed when the § 9.1 restriction is imposed, the notice is given on the day of imposition. A restriction under § 9.8 alone ends at fourteen days and needs no notice under this procedure. Each notice is recorded in the case register under § 13.2.

The operating procedure already carries, by reference from Charter § 15.2, the statement of the protection that applies to a person providing information. No other instruction moves in this pass.

## D. For counsel: proposed classification of each remaining rule

Each section is tagged with the source of its requirement, as proposed for Jeff to confirm or correct. Most of the Charter is Eventus's own design. Where an Eventus choice supports a protection without being a condition of it, the note says so.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **L** | Binding legal duty when its stated conditions apply |
| **P** | Condition of a specified protection, or of a safe harbor Eventus has chosen |
| **E** | Eventus policy choice, including safeguards that support defensibility |
| **S** | Statement describing the law, for counsel to verify; not itself a rule |

The HCQIA notice-and-hearing safe harbor in 42 U.S.C. § 11112(b) is one route to the fairness standard in § 11112(a)(3); failing a particular safe-harbor condition does not by itself defeat that standard. Rows tagged P for that reason are P only because Eventus has chosen the safe harbor.

## E. Verification against the Charter

Every sentence of the pre-simplification operative text was compared, sentence by sentence, with the current text on 2026-11-17. Each sentence removed or changed matched a row in Part B. One rule had been lost: the duty in former §§ 9.6 and 10.9 that "the Committee records the circumstances" of a resignation. It is restored in § 15.9, which now states that the Committee records the circumstances and the determination with its basis. Two cross-references were dropped without loss of meaning: "which § 3.2(b) forbids" in § 9.9 (§ 3.2(b) governs on its own), and "of which there is none" in § 9.7.

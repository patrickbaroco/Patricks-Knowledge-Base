## Design notes (to consider when an item is specified)
- 2026-10-02: **Diabetes companion measures, data limits.** Patrick: medication orders and A1c results can be pulled; treatment orders (fingerstick frequency) and, by implication, the glucose log cannot. Specs for the monotherapy and overtreatment items must be buildable from those two sources alone.
- 2026-09-29: **For the AP/AA specification (design note, not a decision).** A standard requiring the date and outcome of the last gradual dose reduction is met by a note reversing the reduction for a reason unrelated to the target behavior ("began sneezing"). When the spec is written, consider defining the outcome in terms of the documented target behavior: how it responded to the reduction, or another documented clinical reason for the decision that followed. Source: Patrick's stress test, 2026-09-29.
- 2026-10-04: **For the AP/AA specification (design notes from Patrick, not yet a spec).** (1) Patrick wants repetition after a review to be referable on this measure, so the register entry is intended to name Criterion B, not None. That requires the standard to ask only for what the prescriber's own note can carry. (2) Candidate elements: the named target behavior is one that warrants an antipsychotic (distressing psychosis, or agitation endangering the resident or others), not wandering, insomnia, restlessness or calling out alone; what was tried or considered first, drug or non-drug, including pain, sleep, constipation and infection; and, when a reduction is reversed, documented recurrence of the target behavior (what, how often, observed by whom) with the non-drug measures in place. The third generalizes the 9/29 "sneezing" note. (3) Primary care: reaches the physician's periodic visit note under new CQC § 4.7(f) by default. (4) Attribution in buildings where Eventus psychiatry prescribes must be settled before the first review, so reports go to the clinician who owes each element. (5) Expect the first sweep to trip the review-report wave rule (Policy § 5.7) and route to teaching. Source: Patrick, CQC flow discussion 2026-10-04.

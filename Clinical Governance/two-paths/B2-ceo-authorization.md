# Authorization of the Clinical Quality Committee and the Peer Review Committee

*Eventus WholeHealth · Office of the Chief Executive Officer*

---

I authorize the following on behalf of Eventus WholeHealth ("Eventus"), effective [date].

1. **Two committees.** The Clinical Quality Committee and the Peer Review Committee are established as committees of Eventus, and the attached charters are approved. Each is established as a peer review and quality assurance committee of Eventus, for the purpose of furthering quality health care, under federal law and the law of each state in which Eventus practices.

2. **Authority.** The Peer Review Committee decides, on behalf of Eventus, matters concerning the professional competence or conduct of an individual clinician. That includes any action affecting what that clinician may do, and what is reported to the National Practitioner Data Bank and to licensing boards. Its decisions in those matters, signed by the Chair of Clinical Excellence, are the actions of Eventus. The Committee has no authority over staffing, employment, operations or company policy. I take no part in individual cases, and I may withdraw this authority at any time in writing.

3. **Chair and members.** The Chair of Clinical Excellence chairs both committees and appoints their members. No one who holds authority over the employment, compensation or assignment of clinicians sits on the Peer Review Committee or reviews a case.

4. **Health and fitness.** Questions of a clinician's health, impairment or fitness to practice are referred to Human Resources.

5. **Protection.** Eventus will defend and indemnify committee members, hearing panel members, and those assisting them for their good-faith service, and will maintain insurance that covers it. No one will suffer an adverse employment action for good-faith participation.

6. **Reporting and changes.** The Chair meets quarterly with the Chief Medical Officer and Chief Operating Officer and reports in aggregate. I receive the same report, and prompt notice of any litigation arising from a review, any report to the Data Bank, any restriction lasting more than thirty days, and any allegation that the process was not followed. Changes to the charters require my approval.

Eventus will register with the National Practitioner Data Bank as a reporting entity.

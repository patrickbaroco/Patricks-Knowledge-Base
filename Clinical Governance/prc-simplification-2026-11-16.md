# PRC Charter simplification, 2026-11-16

**Result.** The operative text (Articles I to XVI) falls from about 21,650 words to about 18,160, roughly a sixth. Article IX, the pilot, fell by a third; the other articles are denser in rules and gave up less. No substantive decision authority, practitioner right, restriction limit, voting threshold, or external reporting duty changes. One obligation changes location: the day-20 reportability reminder moves from the Charter to a binding operating procedure, whose text is in Part C and takes effect with the shortened Charter. One heading is corrected (§ 9.5), and three passages are cut rather than moved because they made claims the Charter should not carry (§§ 7.2, 7.3, 9.8(g)).

**Method.** Each paragraph was sorted as a rule, a reason, a restatement, or an administrative instruction. Rules stay. Reasons go to the member guide, in its new section *Why the Charter says what it says*, or are cut where they would hurt in any document. Restatements are cut where another section governs. Notices were kept wherever they serve committee oversight or patient safety, whether or not missing one would favor the Practitioner. Deadlines protecting Practitioners, automatic expiry, voting rules and conditions for extending a restriction stay in the Charter.

**Not changed by this pass.** The Open items section, the bracketed defaults, and every rule not listed below. Pre-simplification copy: `_archive/prc-charter-draft-2026-11-16-before-simplification.md`.

## C. Operating procedure: text moved from the Charter

Adopted with the shortened Charter, under the approval in § 16.2, and effective on the same date. The shortened Charter is not adopted without it; otherwise the reminder would have been removed rather than moved.

**OP-1. Thirty-day reportability prompt.** For each restriction under Article IX of the Charter, the Coordinator gives the Chair and the members written notice on the [twentieth] day after imposition, stating the date on which the restriction will have been in effect for thirty days under § 9.4, so that the Committee can determine reportability with counsel under § 15.7 before that date. Where a restriction under § 9.1 follows one under § 9.8 in the same matter, the day is counted from the first imposition under § 9.8(f). The notice is recorded in the case register under § 13.2.

The operating procedure already carries, by reference from Charter § 15.2, the statement of the protection that applies to a person providing information. No other instruction moves in this pass.

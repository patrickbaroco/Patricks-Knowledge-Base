|---|---|---|---|---|
| Colorectal cancer screening in facilities: test choice and eligibility | 2026-09-28 | Patrick | open | Facilities currently use Cologuard. Proposed stance: FIT as the enterprise default, with an eligibility rule based on life expectancy (USPSTF selective 76–85, not routine over 85; AMDA/AGS against screening when life expectancy is under about 10 years). Cologuard Plus is more sensitive and, triennially, yields fewer cumulative false positives than annual FIT, but in a short-horizon LTC population that advantage mostly disappears; FIT is one probe sample at about $20 against a whole-stool collection at about $500, and the annual cycle forces a yearly decision on whether to keep screening. HEDIS and MIPS/ACO CRC measures exclude long-term institutional residents 66+ and frailty with advanced illness. Next: Patrick to email Sandra (2026-09-28). |

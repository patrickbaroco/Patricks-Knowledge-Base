# Committee Time and Cost

*Clinical Quality Committee and Peer Review Committee. Draft for Dr. Rucci, October 27, 2026. Estimated from the hours each member gives, built up member by member.*

## Summary

**The two committees need about 46 to 48 budgeted hours a month, or about six admin days, at a cost of about $44,000 to $47,000 a year.** Each budgeted member gives about half an admin day a month. The three chairs and the four psychiatrists are salaried and add about 51 hours a month at no budgeted cost.

| | Members | Budgeted members | Budgeted hours a month | Admin days a month | Annual cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical Quality Committee | 12 | 7 | 24.5 | 3.1 | $21,100 |
| Peer Review Committee | 8 | 6 | 21.5–23.2 | 2.7–2.9 | $23,300–26,100 |
| **Total** | **20** | **13** | **46.0–47.7** | **5.8–6.0** | **$44,400–47,200** |

Physician time is costed at $50,000 a year per weekly day, about $120 an hour. APC time is estimated at $26,500 a year per weekly day, about $64 an hour, which is one-fifth of the national median nurse practitioner salary of $132,300 (BLS, May 2025). Eventus compensation figures should replace the APC estimate.

## Clinical Quality Committee

**Twelve members, seven of them budgeted, each giving about 3.5 hours a month.** Every member attends a one-hour monthly meeting, prepares for 30 minutes, and reviews charts for about 2 hours. The two psychiatrists review for about 2 hours a week.

| Seat | Members | Hours a month each | Budgeted | Annual cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chair of Clinical Excellence, Chair of Primary Care, Chair of Psychiatry | 3 | 3.5 | Salaried | — |
| Psychiatrists | 2 | about 10 | Salaried | — |
| Primary care physician | 1 | 3.5 | Yes | $5,050 |
| Primary care APCs | 5 | 3.5 | Yes | $13,350 |
| Psychiatric APC | 1 | 3.5 | Yes | $2,700 |
| **Total** | **12** | | **24.5 h budgeted** | **$21,100** |

**Members review the charts themselves.** The audit reads the charts of clinicians whose rates stand apart from their peers'. The committee has about 37 hours of review a month, enough for 150 to 220 charts at 10 to 15 minutes a chart.

**Primary care reviews primary care, and psychiatry reviews psychiatry, as usual practice.** The audit measures charts against a standard the committee adopted, so the charter does not require a peer reviewer. Clinicians will still expect one.

**Five primary care APCs, so no one is asked for more than about 2 hours a month.** The APCs carry full clinical loads. The committee is sized so a slow month for one member does not stall a review cycle.

**One primary care physician seat, with an alternate paid only when called.** The Chair of Clinical Excellence and the Chair of Primary Care screen referrals at the Peer Review Committee, so they take no part in the Clinical Quality Committee's decision to refer a primary care clinician. The primary care physician member is the primary care physician voice in those decisions, and the alternate covers absence or conflict.

**A small planning group protects the clinicians' time.** The three chairs and one member meet monthly with education, Outcomes Centered Health, operations and analytics. They take requests and prepare the committee's business. The full committee then meets for an hour to decide what has been prepared. Decisions stay with the full committee.

Time runs heavier in the weeks before each quarter's EQuIP measures and the quarterly report.

## Peer Review Committee

**Eight voting members, six of them budgeted, each giving about 3 to 6 hours a month at full volume.** The Chair of Clinical Excellence presides without a vote. The Chairs of Primary Care and Psychiatry screen referrals for their service lines and are not members.

| Seat | Members | Hours a month (total) | Budgeted | Annual cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary care physicians | 3 | 10.1–12.4 | Yes | $14,600–17,850 |
| Primary care APCs and psychiatric APC | 3 | 10.8–11.4 | Yes | $8,250–8,700 |
| Psychiatrists | 2 | 5.5–7.2 | Salaried | — |
| **Total** | **8** | | **21.5–23.2 h budgeted** | **$23,300–26,100** |

**Volume is the main uncertainty.** These figures assume ten cases a month. Volume is likely to start lower. Case review scales with volume; the meeting and preparation time does not. At five cases a month, case review costs about half as much.

**Each case is reviewed by two clinicians from the clinician's own service line, for about 50 minutes each.** A physician's care is reviewed by two physicians. An APC's care is reviewed by two clinicians, at least one of them an APC. Peer review judges care against the standard of care, which needs reviewers who practice as the clinician does.

**Three primary care physicians, so a physician's case can still be reviewed after one member steps aside for a conflict.**

**Two primary care APCs, because most primary care referrals are expected to concern APCs.** The expected share is 60 to 70 percent. With one APC member, that person would sit on about two of every three primary care cases.

**Two salaried psychiatrists and one psychiatric APC.** Few psychiatrists see patients within Eventus, so reviews of psychiatrists will be rare. A psychiatric APC's case pairs the APC member with a psychiatrist.

**Membership does not overlap with the Clinical Quality Committee.** A clinician who took part in that committee's decision to refer a matter steps aside when the matter reaches peer review. Separate membership keeps panels intact. It also keeps Clinical Quality Committee members available for hearing panels, which Peer Review Committee members cannot sit on.

## Budgeting by the day

**At a full admin day a month for each budgeted member, the cost is about $101,000 a year.** That is the convention proposed for the BH Committee. It comes to about $48,200 for the Clinical Quality Committee and $52,900 for the Peer Review Committee. The hours above round to about half a day a month for each member. A full day would give headroom for months of high case volume and for the quarters before EQuIP measures and reporting.

## Not included

- The salaried time of the three chairs and four psychiatrists, about 51 hours a month.
- Presiding and screening at the Peer Review Committee.
- Alternates and reviewers called in for conflicts, about $100 per physician call.
- The follow-up chart examinations after an agreed resolution, clinical monitoring of interventions, and interviews.
- Analytics time to produce the clinician-level rates the audit selects from, and the Executive Coordinator.

## Assumptions

- An admin day is 8 hours; a year is 52 weeks.
- Peer Review Committee volume is ten cases a month, split between primary care and psychiatry anywhere from 70/30 to even.
- In primary care, 65 percent of cases concern APCs, and the second reviewer on an APC case is a physician.
- The planning group's fourth member is not costed. If an APC, the cost is about $750 a year.

*Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics median annual wage for nurse practitioners, May 2025, as reported at nurse.org/resources/np-salary-guide.*

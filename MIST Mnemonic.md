## Definition

MIST is a cause-naming schema for encephalopathy / [[Altered Mental Status|altered mental status]] and other "secondary" neurologic syndromes: **M**etabolic, **I**nfectious, **S**tructural, **T**oxin (meds). It answers *what is driving this?* once localization and tempo have narrowed the problem. It is a complement to — and explicitly **not** a part of — Berkowitz's [[Localization × Time Course|time-course buckets]]: in [[CPS 153 - Intermittent Altered Mental Status and Non-Lesional Autoimmune Epilepsy|CPS 153]] MIST was offered by a discussant, not by Berkowitz. Keep the two credited separately — tempo buckets say *how fast*, MIST says *what*. Note the common variant in which **I** is read as *infectious/inflammatory* (folding in autoimmune disease) and **T** as *toxic / metabolic-drug*.

## How it operates

- **M — metabolic.** Electrolytes, glucose, organ failure (renal, hepatic), endocrine derangement.
- **I — infectious (± inflammatory).** CNS (meningitis, encephalitis, abscess) or systemic; many use this letter to also carry autoimmune/antibody-mediated disease.
- **S — structural.** Mass, stroke, hemorrhage, hydrocephalus — the imaging question.
- **T — toxin / meds.** Intoxication, withdrawal, or a culprit drug — the prompt to name the *specific* agent rather than waving at "toxic-metabolic."
- **Use it to force specificity, and pair it with tempo.** MIST names the driver; [[Illness Tempo|tempo]] sets the pace — run them as two tools, not one.
- **It generalizes past AMS.** The same M/I/S/T split organizes other "secondary vs primary" problems, e.g. [[Myoclonus|myoclonus]] (secondary MIST causes vs a primary neurologic disorder).

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **MIST applied to altered mental status.** A discussant runs the MIST buckets on an undifferentiated AMS consult, complementing the tempo-based approach. ([[CPS 153 - Intermittent Altered Mental Status and Non-Lesional Autoimmune Epilepsy|CPS 153]])
- **The structural/occult driver.** Persistent AMS drives the search through MIST to a structural-infectious source (a spinal epidural abscess). ([[RLR 033 - Persistent Altered Mental Status and Spinal Epidural Abscess|RLR 033]])
- **Metabolic and toxic drivers in a confused patient.** The workup of altered sensorium and polyuria walks the metabolic/toxic branches of the schema. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])
- **MIST beyond AMS — myoclonus.** Myoclonus is split into secondary causes (metabolic, infectious, structural, toxin/meds) versus a primary neurologic disorder. ([[RLR 037 - Episodic Lightheadedness and Functional Neurological Disorder|RLR 037]])

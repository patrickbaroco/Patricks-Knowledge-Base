## My review

**Haunting.** I rate the books I read. It takes no time to click the number of stars. But a few rate the time of a review, in the hopes that others will read this book. It is a page-turner, and a book you finish and wish it hadn’t ended. I rate it as one of the best I’ve read, and I know I will read it again.

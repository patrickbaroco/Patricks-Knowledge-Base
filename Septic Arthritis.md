Evergreen concept note. Septic arthritis is a joint-destroying emergency whose diagnosis often turns not on certainty but on a risk-tolerance decision made against the far more common crystal disease. RLR / Clinical Problem Solvers teaching points are cited per episode; general clinical detail is scaffolding.

## Definition and Microbiology
Bacterial infection within the joint space, seeded hematogenously, by direct inoculation, or from adjacent osteomyelitis.

- **Organisms** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]) — *Staphylococcus aureus* (most common), streptococci, and *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* in young sexually active patients (often migratory polyarthralgia, tenosynovitis, dermatitis). Gram-negatives in the elderly, IV drug users, and immunocompromised.
- **Prosthetic joints and endovascular devices** shift the base rate toward infection ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).

## Risk Factors
Pre-existing joint disease (RA, OA, gout), prosthetic joint, older age, diabetes, immunosuppression, IV drug use, skin breakdown/cellulitis, recent joint procedure, and bacteremia from any source.

## Clinical Features
- Acute mono- (occasionally oligo-) articular pain, swelling, warmth, erythema.
- **Refusal to bear weight; pain with both active and passive motion** ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]) — see [[Active vs Passive Range of Motion]]. Fever is unreliable and often absent.

## Diagnosis and the Risk-Tolerance Decision
- **[[Synovial Fluid Analysis|Synovial fluid]] is central** — Gram stain (specific, ~50% sensitive), culture (~80% sensitive), and WBC count as a probability-of-sepsis estimator rising past ~50,000 and skyrocketing above ~100,000 (JAMA Rational Clinical Examination) ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).
- **[[Base Rate|Base rate]] favors crystals; morbidity lives in sepsis** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]). Because every assay is imperfect, the decision to treat as septic is a **[[Bayesian Reasoning|risk-tolerance]] judgment scaled to joint count, systemic illness, and cell count** — a sicker or multi-joint patient pushes you to cover infection empirically while awaiting cultures.
- **Coexistence with crystal disease** — a positive crystal never exonerates you from infection; the two occur together in a minority ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]], [[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).
- **Time and treatment response are diagnostic tests** — serial negative cultures plus resolution on crystal therapy retrospectively confirm gout ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]). See [[Treatment as a Diagnostic Test]].

## Management
- **Joint drainage** (repeated aspiration, arthroscopic, or open washout) PLUS **empiric IV antibiotics** targeting likely organisms, narrowed by culture.
- Steroids for a concurrent gout flare are safe **alongside** antibiotics ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).
- Prosthetic joint infection requires surgical/ID co-management.


> [!tldr] TL;DR
> - Inhaled therapy selection in COPD is driven by symptoms, using the GOLD ABE framework
> - Inhaled corticosteroids are third-line therapy and should generally be avoided when blood eosinophils are below 300

---

## GOLD ABE Groups

Classification is based on two symptom domains: dyspnea burden and exacerbation history.

**Dyspnea scoring** — use mMRC (fast, practical) or CAT (more detailed):

**Exacerbation severity:**
- Moderate = requires rescue inhaler and/or oral steroids
- Severe = requires hospitalization

> [!note] ABE Group Definitions
> - **Group A** — 0–1 moderate exacerbations, mMRC 0–1 (no daily dyspnea)
> - **Group B** — 0–1 moderate exacerbations, mMRC ≥ 2 (daily dyspnea)
> - **Group E** — more than 1 moderate exacerbation, or any severe exacerbation

---

## Treatment by Group

**Drug class definitions:**
- **LABA** (Long-Acting Beta Agonist): salmeterol, formoterol, vilanterol
- **LAMA** (Long-Acting Muscarinic Antagonist): tiotropium, umeclidinium, aclidinium, glycopyrrolate
- **ICS** (Inhaled Corticosteroid): fluticasone, mometasone, budesonide, beclomethasone

> [!example] Treatment Algorithm
> - **Group A** → LAMA monotherapy (preferred) or LABA
> - **Group B** → LABA + LAMA (dual therapy)
> - **Group E** → LABA + LAMA; add ICS only if blood eosinophils ≥ 300

> [!note] Inhaler Options
**LAMA monotherapy:**
>- Tiotropium (Spiriva)
>- Aclidinium (Tudorza)
>- Umeclidinium (Incruse Ellipta)
>
>**LABA monotherapy:**
>- Salmeterol (Serevent)
>
>**LABA/LAMA combination:**
>- Umeclidinium/vilanterol (Anoro Ellipta)
>
>**Triple therapy (LABA + LAMA + ICS):**
>- Fluticasone/umeclidinium/vilanterol (Trelegy)
>- Budesonide/glycopyrrolate/formoterol (Breztri)

## Thoughts and Takeaways

> [!warning] ICS Overuse in COPD
> Inhaled corticosteroids increase pneumonia risk and offer limited benefit in most people with COPD. Check the absolute eosinophil count on the CBC before continuing or starting ICS therapy.
> 
> - **Eosinophils < 300** → stop the ICS
>   - On LABA + ICS (e.g., Advair)? Switch to LAMA monotherapy (Spiriva, Tudorza, or Incruse Ellipta)
>   - On triple therapy? Step down to Anoro Ellipta
> - **Eosinophils ≥ 300** → ICS (and triple therapy) appropriate

> [!tip] De-escalation After a Stable Year
> If a person with COPD has gone 12 months without an exacerbation:
> - On dual therapy → step down to LAMA monotherapy
> - On monotherapy → consider discontinuing the inhaler and monitoring

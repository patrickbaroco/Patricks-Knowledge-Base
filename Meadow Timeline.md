## Phase 1 — Planning & Ordering (July–August)

- [ ] Walk the full conversion area; flag wet spots, shade zones, compaction — noted 2026-07-25: front is shaded, gully is part-sun/part-shade (dappled from small trees, but strong morning/early-afternoon sun), and a small fruit-tree strip sits at the top of the drainfield
- [ ] Mark mowed path loop and gulley anchor point
- [ ] Decide on final seed mix (little bluestem, switchgrass, black-eyed Susan, butterfly milkweed + nurse crop)
- [ ] Order seed from Roundstone or Ernst — do this early, popular mixes sell out [[Materials Quantities for Meadow Project]]
- [ ] Coordinate with lawn mowing contractor: need grass mowed short before each herbicide application

## Phase 2 — Vegetation Kill (Late August – Mid September)

- [ ] **Application 1:** Glyphosate, late August — mow short first, needs 24–48 dry hours after
- [ ] Wait 3–4 weeks
- [ ] **Application 2:** Glyphosate, mid September — same protocol

> [!warning] Calendar check
> Protect these two weekends now. The kill window is narrow and weather-dependent.

## Phase 3 — Site Prep (Late September – Mid October)

- [ ] Assess thatch after second kill — rake or rent power rake/dethatcher if heavy
- [ ] Any rough grading near gulley
- [ ] Clear and mark path loop edges
- [ ] Order/plant trees

> [!warning] Deadline
> Site prep must be complete by mid-October. Don't let away weekends compress this phase.

## Phase 4 — Dormant Seeding (Mid October – Early December)

- [ ] Monitor nighttime temps — seed when consistently below 40°F
- [ ] Broadcast seed by hand or push spreader
- [ ] Rake lightly for soil contact
- [ ] No irrigation needed — let freeze-thaw cycles work

> [!note]
> Typical window for rural Virginia: mid-to-late October. Can slide to November if October is warm or wet.

## Phase 5 — Year One Establishment (Spring–Summer, Following Year)

- [ ] Expect weedy, sparse appearance — this is normal
- [ ] Mow everything to 6–8 inches once or twice in spring to suppress annuals — **John Deere X758 deck only adjusts 1–5.25 in.**, so set to max (5.25 in.) as the practical target instead of the full 6–8 in.
- [ ] Do not skip spring mowing — perennials are rooting, not yet flowering
- [ ] Expect meaningful flowering in Year 2

> [!tip] The patience phase
> Resist the urge to intervene beyond the spring mow. The meadow is working underground.

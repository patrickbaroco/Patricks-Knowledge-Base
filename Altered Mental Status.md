## Definition

Altered mental status is a final common pathway, not a diagnosis — nearly every organ system can produce it. The most powerful way to make it tractable (Aaron Berkowitz) is [[Localization × Time Course|localization × time course]]: confirm the brain is the site, then sort the differential by **tempo**. Two gates precede the differential — first *neurologic vs non-neurologic*, then the *time-course bucket* — and only after those does the list of specific diseases become short enough to work.

## How it operates

- **Gate 1 — neurologic vs non-neurologic.** Because any organ system can cause AMS, decide first whether this is a brain problem at all. If you invoke "toxic-metabolic," name *which* toxin or metabolite rather than signing off — a complementary cause-naming schema is [[MIST Mnemonic|MIST]] (metabolic, infectious, structural, toxin), a separate tool, *not* part of Berkowitz's framework.
- **Gate 2 — bucket by tempo.** *Instant*: vascular (stroke — "just add water"), seizure/post-ictal, toxic-metabolic, trauma. *Acute* (hours-days): infection (meningitis, encephalitis, abscess), inflammatory/demyelinating. *Subacute* (weeks-months): neoplasm/mass, antibody-mediated/autoimmune/paraneoplastic, indolent infection (fungal, TB, HIV), the rare rapidly-progressive prion dementia. *Chronic*: neurodegenerative. Toxic-metabolic fits *every* bucket.
- **"Intermittent" is a loud word.** Intermittent AMS points at [[Seizure|seizure]]; ask whether the patient is *normal* between spells (discrete post-ictal recovery) or *declining* between them (a superimposed progressive process). Not all seizures convulse.
- **New-onset seizure in an older adult + a non-lesional MRI → antibody-mediated.** Shift to [[Autoimmune Encephalitis|autoimmune/antibody-mediated]] disease (anti-LGI1, anti-GABA-B, anti-NMDA), hunting for but not requiring a cancer.
- **Match the confirmatory test to the phenomenon.** When the problem is *spells*, continuous video EEG — not a single cross-sectional study — is the test that captures it.

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **The approach in full.** Localize to brain, bucket by tempo, and let the word *intermittent* pivot the case from generic AMS to non-lesional autoimmune epilepsy. ([[CPS 153 - Intermittent Altered Mental Status and Non-Lesional Autoimmune Epilepsy|CPS 153]])
- **Name the toxin/metabolite.** A drug — ertapenem — is the culprit metabolite; the case is the reminder not to stop at "toxic-metabolic." ([[RLR 024 - Confusion and Ertapenem-Induced Encephalopathy|RLR 024]])
- **A persistent deficit forces the structural/occult search.** AMS that will not clear demands a hunt for a structural driver (here a spinal epidural abscess) rather than settling on metabolism. ([[RLR 033 - Persistent Altered Mental Status and Spinal Epidural Abscess|RLR 033]])
- **Episodic neurologic status as its own puzzle.** Recurrent, triggered, remitting neurologic episodes with normal structural imaging push toward toxic-metabolic, hereditary, and ultimately functional explanations. ([[RLR 037 - Episodic Lightheadedness and Functional Neurological Disorder|RLR 037]])

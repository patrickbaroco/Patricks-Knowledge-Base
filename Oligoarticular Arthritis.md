Evergreen concept note. When two to four joints are inflamed, intra-articular and systemic disease become more likely while the yield of tapping any one joint falls. RLR / Clinical Problem Solvers teaching points are cited per episode; general clinical detail is scaffolding.

## Definition
Oligoarticular arthritis involves 2–4 joints; polyarticular involves 5 or more. The joint *count* is itself diagnostic information that reshapes both the differential and the value of arthrocentesis.

## How Joint Count Shifts the Probabilities
- **Intra-articular likelihood goes UP** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]). Simultaneous crystal or tendon disease in anatomically far-apart joints is improbable, so multiple inflamed joints point toward a **systemic, intra-articular** process rather than a local mechanical one.
- **Arthrocentesis yield goes DOWN** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]). If a systemic disease (e.g., lupus) is inflaming every joint, tapping one tells you little about *why*. More joints → think systemic; expect less from the needle.
- **Analogy** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]): many abnormal cells in the peripheral *blood* point to the *marrow* — the systemic source — just as many inflamed joints point to a systemic driver rather than one joint.

## Differential Diagnosis
- **Crystalline** — polyarticular [[Gout]] and [[CPPD]] can flare in several joints at once, often provoked by volume shifts or illness.
- **Infectious** — disseminated gonococcal infection (migratory oligoarthritis, tenosynovitis, dermatitis), other bacteremias, and viral arthritides (parvovirus, hepatitis B, HIV).
- **Autoimmune / inflammatory** — rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, psoriatic and other spondyloarthritides; sarcoidosis.
- **Reactive arthritis** — post-enteric or post-urogenital infection (classically asymmetric lower-limb oligoarthritis).

## Workup
- **Still rule out [[Septic Arthritis|septic arthritis]] first** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]) — even polyarticular presentations can harbor infection, and a septic joint among several is easy to miss.
- **Tap the most accessible effused joint** for [[Synovial Fluid Analysis|synovial fluid]] (Gram stain, crystals, cell count), recognizing the reduced yield.
- **Layer in systemic serologies** guided by the DDx (RF/anti-CCP, ANA, HLA-B27, urate) and cultures; use pattern (symmetric vs asymmetric, large vs small joint, migratory) to prioritize.

## Management Principles
- Treat the identified process; when septic arthritis cannot be excluded in a sick or multi-joint patient, the threshold to **cover empirically for infection rises with joint count and systemic illness** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).
- Use [[Treatment as a Diagnostic Test|time and treatment response]] as confirmatory tests when initial assays are ambiguous.

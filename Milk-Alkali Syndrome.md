Evergreen concept note. Milk-alkali (calcium-alkali) syndrome across the hypercalcemia episodes. Teaching points cited per episode.

## The triad and the base rate

- **Suspect it with the triad of [[Hypercalcemia|hypercalcemia]], metabolic *alkalosis*, and AKI.** Milk-alkali fell below 1% of hypercalcemia after H2 blockers/PPIs replaced Dr. Sippy's powder-and-cream, but has resurged with over-the-counter calcium carbonate for bone health to become the **third most common cause (~10–12%)**. ([[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])
- **The bicarbonate direction is the tell.** The expected [[Hypercalcemia|hypercalcemia]] → [[Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|nephrogenic DI]] → volume contraction → AKI usually yields a non-gap *acidosis*; a *high* bicarbonate instead points to an exogenous calcium/alkali load a contracted kidney can no longer excrete. A coexisting acidosis argues *against* milk-alkali. ([[RLR 014 - Hypercalcemia and Immobilization|RLR 014]], [[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])

## Pathophysiology (the vicious cycle)

- The hypercalcemia trigger itself is not fully understood, but once present it causes nephrogenic DI two ways — reduced aquaporins and blockade of the Na-K-2Cl transporter in the ascending loop (the loop-diuretic site). The resulting volume contraction drives bicarbonate retention; add ongoing calcium-carbonate intake and the cycle of worsening alkalosis and kidney injury self-amplifies. ([[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])

## Management and its trap

- **Hydrate and hold the alkali; do not reach for a bisphosphonate.** These patients correct fast, and once the calcium falls [[Parathyroid Hormone|PTH]] un-suppresses within minutes; a [[Zoledronic Acid|bisphosphonate]] that has driven calcium into bone will then produce frank *hypocalcemia*. Corollary clue: an unexpected hypocalcemia after treating a hypercalcemia is itself diagnostic. ([[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])
- **Rapid resolution with hydration supports it; a rebound does not exonerate an ingestion but retires milk-alkali as the *sole* cause** — you can put an [[Hickam's Dictum|AND, not an OR]] (milk-alkali layered on [[Vitamin D Toxicity|vitamin D toxicity]] as a second hit). ([[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])
- Historically a "distant third" behind hyperparathyroidism and malignancy on the outpatient base-rate list. ([[RLR 007 - Hypercalcemia|RLR 007]], [[RLR 014 - Hypercalcemia and Immobilization|RLR 014]])

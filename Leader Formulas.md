# Leader Formulas — Maxima Clear & Ultragreen

Maxima Clear/Ultragreen for butt and mid sections on every leader. Swap to Rio or Cortland nylon for the tippet on dry fly leaders only — subsurface leaders (nymph, streamer) stay full Maxima.

Clear vs. Chameleon: functionally similar stiffness. Clear suits clear/spring creek water (my typical conditions); Chameleon better for stained/tannic water.

## General Dry Fly — 9'

- 30" Clear .017 (25 lb)
- 20" Clear .015 (20 lb)
- 20" Clear .013 (15 lb)
- 15" Ultragreen .011 (12 lb)
- 10" Ultragreen .009 (8 lb)
- 20" Rio/Cortland .006 (5X, ~4–5 lb)

## Ultra-Delicate Dry Fly — 9'–10'

For spring creeks, selective risers, flat/glassy water, small flies (#18–24).

- 30" Clear .015 (15 lb)
- 20" Clear .013 (12 lb)
- 15" Clear .011 (10 lb)
- 15" Ultragreen .009 (8 lb)
- 12" Ultragreen .007 (6 lb)
- 24" Rio/Cortland .005 (6X, ~3.5–4 lb)
- 18" Rio/Cortland .004 (7X, ~2.5–3 lb)

Notes: long, gradual taper to dissipate energy and land tippet with slack. Degrease tippet entirely; grease butt/mid if riding high in film. Save for still conditions — collapses in wind.

## Nymphing (tight-line / indicator)

- 20" Clear .017 (25 lb)
- 15" Clear .015 (20 lb)
- 15" Clear .013 (15 lb)
- 15" Ultragreen .011 (12 lb)
- 24–36" Ultragreen .009 (8 lb) tippet, straight through (no further taper — want sink, not turnover)

Adjust finer to .007 (6 lb) for smaller nymphs or more finicky fish; heavier to .011 (10 lb) for bigger weighted patterns or fishing near structure.

## Streamer / Big Water

- 24" Clear .022 (30 lb)
- 18" Clear .020 (25 lb)
- 18" Clear .017 (20 lb)
- 24–30" Ultragreen .015 (12–15 lb) tippet, short and stout

## Windy Day / Heavy Turnover — 7.5' stout

- 24" Clear .022 (30 lb)
- 18" Clear .020 (25 lb)
- 18" Clear .017 (20 lb)
- 18" Clear .015 (15 lb)
- 12" Ultragreen .009 (8 lb) tippet

## Small Stream / Delicate Presentation — 7.5'–8'

- 18" Clear .015 (15 lb)
- 15" Clear .013 (12 lb)
- 12" Ultragreen .011 (10 lb)
- 10" Ultragreen .009 (8 lb)
- 18" Ultragreen .006–.005 (5 lb–4 lb) tippet

## X-to-Diameter Reference (nylon, for Rio/Cortland dry fly tippet)

| X-size | Diameter | Approx. lb test |
|---|---|---|
| 3X | .008" | 8–9 lb |
| 4X | .007" | 6–7 lb |
| 5X | .006" | 4–5 lb |
| 6X | .005" | 3.5–4 lb |
| 7X | .004" | 2.5–3 lb |
| 8X | .003" | 1.5–2 lb |

## General Notes

- Blood-knot all sections; hold each to ~60–75% of prior diameter.
- Rebuild the terminal 2–3 ft rather than the whole leader.
- Maxima runs thick for its rated strength vs. modern nylons — fine for butt/mid, but why tippet gets swapped out for dry fly work.
- Fluorocarbon not used for dry fly tippet — denser than water, wants to sink.

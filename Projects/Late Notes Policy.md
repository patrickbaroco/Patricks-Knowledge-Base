## Context

Notes signed after two business days are paid at 50%. Craig Mitchell cited the policy when he resigned, and Paul Tompkins said much the same. I don't think one policy explains either departure, but the cut reads as Eventus profiting from documentation that is hard, and it stops working as an incentive once the money is gone.

Two proposals are now on the table. Mine, sent to Cary on 10/19, keeps the cut and adds a quarterly on-time bonus: 1% at 95% on time, 2% at 97.5%, 3% at 99%, possibly budget-neutral inside EQuIP, with eligibility forfeited by any note later than seven days. It is aimed at the good clinician who occasionally falls behind; chronic lateness stays a discipline and peer-review problem. Steve Colvin's, sent 10/28, drops the financial penalty for an EQuIP multiplier tied to the share of notes closed within 48 hours, which would move enforcement from HR to clinical governance.

Colvin's incomplete-notes escalation procedure (10/15) is the operational half: tiered, with weighted notes and benching at step 3. Cary agreed on 10/24 to review it.

Registered as a project at the Monday task-to-project review on 2026-11-03, after carrying through the W43 and W44 Handoffs.

## Decisions

- **2026-10-15: the PRC stays out of open notes.** Documentation delinquency reaches the PRC only on a nexus to patient welfare or when it persists after Management has acted and Management refers it (PRC § 14.2). Repeated removal from the field is a cumulative mandatory referral (PRC § 4.3(a)). CQC § 7.7 covers a request for help with documentation. See [[Clinical Governance]].
- My view: delinquency is usually workflow and time management, and caseload is standard across the company.

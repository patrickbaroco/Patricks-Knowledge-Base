# Clinical Excellence — Cadence Map

Companion to [[Chair of Clinical Excellence]]. The role runs on influence, not authority, so the meeting structure is the operating structure. This note defines it.

## The monthly loop

The design principle: **analytics leads, councils decide, education translates.** Cadence is built so each step has its input in hand before it starts. Run it on a monthly cycle keyed to whenever Jacob can reliably deliver data.

| Week | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Metric pack drops. Working session to interpret it — what moved, what's noise, what's a real signal. | Jacob Lindbert (Analytics) |
| **Week 2** | Pre-read built from the pack. Consults on anything heading to council: medication signals with Monica, education implications with Will. Peer review case selection runs against the agreed methodology. | Monica Leriger, Will Sampson, Clinical Quality team |
| **Week 3** | **Clinical Quality Council** meets — decisions get made. **Peer Review Council** meets (recommend alternating months to start; see charter). | Councils |
| **Week 4** | Translation. Decisions become pathway edits, education modules, and a communication to the Regional CMOs. Report out to Sandeep. | Will Sampson, Regional CMOs, Sandeep |

If week 1 slips, everything downstream slips. Protect it.

> [!warning] The failure mode to watch
> Councils that meet before the data arrives fill the hour with anecdote. Two consecutive anecdote meetings and the standing members quietly stop preparing. Cancel a council meeting rather than hold one without a pack.

## Standing roster

**Tier 1 — the engine room.** These four determine whether anything gets produced. Highest frequency, protected on the calendar.

Note the asymmetry: three of these four are relationships you influence. The fourth — the Clinical Quality team — is one you direct. It is the only standing capacity in this map that can be *assigned* work. Treat that accordingly; it's scarce.

| Who | Function | Cadence | Purpose | What you bring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sandeep Patel** | CMO — reports to | Biweekly, 30 min | Priority alignment, air cover, escalation, early read on political weather | One decision needed, one risk, one win |
| **Jacob Lindbert** | Analytics | Weekly 30 min during the metric-pack build; monthly 45 min once stable | Build and interpret the metric pack. Definition fights get settled here, not in council | The question you need answered, not the report you want built |

Council decisions tracked to closure, regulatory and survey interface, measure reporting, QAPI support | The open decision log and what slipped since last week |

## What deliberately does not get a standing call

Cadence sprawl is the characteristic failure of a new enterprise role. Each of these is a real relationship that does not need a recurring slot:

- Individual market operations — that's Rahul's forum, attend by invitation
- IT/EHR — project-based, not standing
- Individual facility QAPI — attend selectively as a signal-gathering exercise, not as a standing obligation
- Anything where you'd be the fourth physician in the room

## Meeting design notes

**The Sandeep 1:1 is the most important 30 minutes.** Structure it the same way every time: one decision you need from him, one risk you're flagging early, one thing that's working. Consistency of format is what turns a check-in into a channel.

**The Regional CMO forum is a coalition, not an audience.** Bring drafts, not decisions. A standard that Regional CMOs shaped gets implemented; a standard they received gets acknowledged and ignored. Budget the first three sessions almost entirely to listening.

**Never let Analytics work without a question.** The failure mode with a good analytics partner is a beautiful dashboard nobody uses. Every request to Jacob should take the form of a decision you're trying to make.

**Protect clinical time.** The credibility this role runs on is that you still round. Put it on the calendar as immovable before the cadence calls fill in around it — see the open question in [[Chair of Clinical Excellence]].

## Rollout

Send Tier 1 invitations first, this week. Tier 2 the following week. Let the councils wait until their charters are approved — a council that convenes before it knows its own decision rights spends its first three meetings inventing them.

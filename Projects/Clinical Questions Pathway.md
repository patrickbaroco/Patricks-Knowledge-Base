## Context

Build a real route for a clinician with a clinical question to get an answer — owned by the **Clinical Quality Committee**, registered 2026-09-06 as a project under [[Clinical Quality Framework]].

Two forums already occupy this ground and neither is designed for it. The **Experienced Clinician Roundtable** (Tuesdays 4–5pm on Teams, 570 invited — a blast, not a meeting) is in practice where people ask random questions; Cary's read on 2026-09-03 was that this is *fine*, that it might be better as a monitored Teams channel, and that **nobody knows what it currently is**. **Clinical Director Office Hours** is the other standing all-hands slot, and "restructure office hours meeting" has sat in `Projects/_Inbox` unsorted since 2026-07-21. This project absorbs both: it names what the roundtable is, replaces it with something built for the purpose, and closes out the office-hours item rather than leaving it to be restructured on its own.

Why it belongs to the CQC rather than to education or to peer review: a question asked and answered is the smallest unit of the same loop the Committee runs at scale — measure, teach, audit. The questions people actually ask are also the cheapest signal available about where the standard is not landing, and today that signal is thrown away on a Teams call nobody logs.

## Open questions

- **Synchronous, asynchronous, or both?** A monitored Teams channel and a standing hour answer different needs — the channel catches the question at the moment it is asked, the hour catches the ones nobody thinks to type. Cary floated the channel; nothing is decided.
- **Who answers, and what makes them the answerer?** No distinguished physicians exist and there is no mechanism to create them (Peer Review, 2026-09-03), so the obvious roster is not available.
- **What happens to an answer once given?** An answer that helps one person and evaporates is the same distribution failure as the clinical pathways — 19 APCs polled, 3 knew where the pathways were, 1 had read more than one. Answers should feed [[Clinical Handbook]] and the education agenda, which is the tie to the education goal.
- **Is a question ever a signal that needs a different route?** A question that reveals a gap in someone's practice is not a peer review referral and must not turn into one, or the channel dies on first use. Where the line sits is unresolved.
- **Does this survive Ben?** The CQC is unchartered pending Ben's green-light (Cary previews Wed 2026-09-09, see [[Clinical Governance]]). Diagnosis work is ungated; standing up a new forum in the Committee's name is not.

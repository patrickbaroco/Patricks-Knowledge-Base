# Declutter Decision Rules — Cheat Sheet

*Print this page and keep it on hand during work sessions.* Linked from [[Whole-House Declutter & Reorganization]].

---

## Every item, every room

Sort into one of four bins: **Keep** · **Donate/Sell** · **Trash/Recycle** · **Undecided**

Undecided is not a resting place — it moves to the next room's "process" pile, and by the attic/basement phase it must resolve to one of the other three.

---

## Kitchen / Pantry

- Work **one zone at a time** (don't open every cabinet at once)
- **Single-use gadget test**: used fewer than 2 times this year → out
- **One layer deep**: if you can't see it without moving something else, it doesn't fit — reduce or re-store

## Clothes

- **Hanger-flip trick**: turn all hangers backward; after 6 months, anything still backward hasn't been worn → out
- Sort **by category across the entire closet** (all shirts together, all pants together), not shelf-by-shelf
- "Fits someday" items → **one labeled bin** with a written revisit date; if not revisited by then, it goes

## Papers

- Three piles only: **shred** / **scan-then-shred** / **keep physical**
- Keep physical = fireproof box, **true originals only** (deeds, wills, birth certificates, current tax records)
- Everything else gets scanned into `Household/Documents` with consistent naming before shredding
- See [[Whole-House Declutter - Paper Retention Reference]] for how long to keep what

## Toys

- **Rotation model**: active set out, one rotation set boxed and out of sight
- A boxed set **untouched across two rotations** → donate
- Best purged **without young kids present**; include older kids in decisions
- Sentimental keepsakes go to the fixed-size bin below, not the general toy bin

## Sentimental items (any room)

- **One fixed-size bin per person** — no exceptions to the size
- Bin full? Something old leaves before something new comes in

---

## When organizing (after a room's purge is done)

- **One home per category** — every category of item has exactly one place it lives
- **Zone by function**, not by object type (e.g. "breakfast zone" not "all mugs, all bowls")
- **Container the boundary, not the item** — the container's size is the limit; when it's full, something leaves
- **One inbox bin** per room for things without a home yet — process it regularly, don't let it become permanent storage

---

## Sustain

- Seasonal re-pass, spring and fall
- **One-in-one-out**: new item in → equivalent item out

- 2026-09-03: **The COO asked for the handbook and named three uses for it.** See [[Cary Trainor Connect — Clinical Governance, 2026-09-03]]. This started as a conversation about why the clinical pathways don't reach anyone and ended with Cary Trainor asking directly: *"How can I get my hands on your full work in progress?"* The shape both landed on:
    1. **The handbook is the resource** — *this is your resource, all of our NPs, all of our docs*.
    2. **Chapters become the Wednesday didactics** — turned into PowerPoints, with the handbook as ground truth so the seminars and the written material say the same thing.
    3. **The orientation section becomes new hire orientation.**

    **Why (3) matters most: there is currently no orientation to long-term care at all.** Cary has been through new hire training twice, and by day three of a three-day program clinicians are still using the wrong terms for the type of facility they are about to walk into. Patrick's matching example: a PA who has worked in this space *for years* asking **"What is Part A?"** What Cary specifically values about the chapters is that **they are written for someone with no background** — *"it's so easy to assume you were born with all this knowledge, and you forget that somebody actually had to teach you that."*

    She wants to rebuild new hire training around the BCG model, where training arrives the exact week you hit the problem it addresses — tools at the start, client skills at month three, managerial at month six. *"We have such an opportunity there."*

- 2026-09-03: **State of the manuscript, as described to Cary.** Everything lives in personal Obsidian with frontmatter tags. A **regulatory / operations / orientation section** exists covering: the long-term care ecosystem, the ACO, MDS and care planning, CMS star ratings, quality measures, the F-tags clinicians encounter most, survey readiness for clinical staff, the clinician's role, and prehospitalization. **Cardiology is the most complete** (heart failure is "pretty squared away," with both a chapter-length version and a one-page *doorway version*); **GI is nearly empty** — *"you can also tell what my interests are."* Most chapters have **never been read by anyone.** Cary already has the table of contents and it is roughly current; falls has been added since.

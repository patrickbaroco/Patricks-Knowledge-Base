## What a microtransition is, and why the study exists

A **microtransition** is a minor care transition — an outpatient clinic visit, dialysis, imaging, a procedure, a room change, or a non-medical outing such as a family meal, a wedding or a funeral. The lab's argument is that these carry the same care-coordination risk as facility-to-facility transitions and get none of the same attention.

The number driving the project: **50% of clinic visits produce no after-visit summary or progress note back to the facility**, while facility staff remain responsible for carrying out whatever orders were generated at that visit.

The **MASS tool** is an enhanced sign-out/sign-in form completed by whoever escorts a resident out of the building — staff, van driver, or family. It captures trip characteristics (escort, destination, food consumed) and trip-related problems (skin tears, motion sickness, cancelled appointments), and the intervention is that a copy reaches the nursing team on return.

## Design

- **Stepped wedge, not a control arm.** Every facility gets the intervention and uses the MASS tool for the whole study; what is randomized is *when* the nursing team starts receiving copies of the form — at 3, 5 or 7 months. No facility is a control, and this is the answer to the first objection any DON will raise.
- **Duration:** 12 months, plus 1-2 onboarding months.
- **Burden:** the published figure is ≤90 additional seconds per sign-out versus current process. Nursing staff complete no additional documentation. The study team does all chart review.
- **Training:** ~30 minutes per staff member, delivered by an assigned Research Coordinator, virtual or in person, with quarterly refreshers; required for van drivers, escorts, recreation staff, receptionists and nursing.
- **Data:** REDCap, HIPAA-compliant, IRB waiver granted. Facility owns its own patient data; no facility is named in publication without permission.
- **Money:** grant-funded. Facilities pay nothing.
- **QAPI is the vehicle throughout** — introduction, progress updates, and end-of-study results all run through the facility's QAPI committee.
- **IRB status:** approved for data collection with the MASS tool; **approval for the intervention component is pending.**

## What a candidate facility looks like

From the meeting and the slide deck, in the order they matter:

1. **A SNF Eventus already covers**, with an Eventus medical director who can invite the study team to QAPI.
2. **An engaged DON.** Canter named this twice; it is the difference between a study and a form nobody fills in.
3. **Someone at the reception desk roughly 9-5.** Ideal, not required — the FAQ confirms they have run sites with full-time, part-time and no receptionist.
4. **Read-only EMR access.** PointClickCare is ideal (every current site uses it) but the team can learn any system.
5. Real outpatient trip volume — dialysis, clinic visits, recreational outings — or there is nothing to measure.

Patrick's own screen, not the study team's: **not a building in the middle of an ownership change, an administrator vacancy, or an active survey problem.**

## Context

Rebuild how clinical education works at Eventus — a **CQC goal**, registered 2026-09-06 under [[Clinical Quality Framework]]. Typed as a goal from the start rather than as a project, because it has no next step and won't have one: it is the thing that concrete education projects will hang off. `projects:` is empty today and gets entries as they become real, following the precedent set when the Framework was re-typed on 2026-09-04.

The case for it, in the COO's words on 2026-09-03: ***"you don't have quality if you don't have good education"*** — education and quality pair hand in glove, and today they are not synchronized.

**What is wrong now, from three independent directions:**

- **The channel doesn't reach anyone.** 19 Virginia APCs polled: **3 knew where the clinical pathways were at all, 1 had ever looked at more than one.** The cause is distribution, not content — PDFs on a wiki that is hard to reach and miserable to read on a phone, which is where clinicians actually look.
- **The dedicated slot is unattended.** The ACO holds the fourth Wednesday of each month for its own education slot, and **nobody is jumping up and down to attend the ACO education meeting.** Standalone sessions built around a payer's agenda lose to didactics people already attend.
- **There is no orientation to long-term care at all.** Cary has been through new hire training twice; by day three of a three-day program clinicians still use the wrong terms for the type of facility they are about to walk into. Matching example: a PA who has worked in this space *for years* asking **"What is Part A?"**

**The shape of the replacement, as already argued to both Sandra and Cary:** education folds into the didactics people already attend rather than standing alone; the six-month initiative saturates every channel at once — *"Right now our mission is heart failure. We should just bombard people with heart failure"* — across seminars, Thursday meetings, short newsletters, one-page doorway versions of handbook chapters, and possibly an iPhone app; and every channel points at the same content, which is [[Clinical Handbook]]. New hire training gets rebuilt on the BCG model, where training arrives the week you hit the problem it addresses.

## Decisions

- **2026-09-06: registered as a goal, not a project.** No status, no next action, no `todoist_label` — a goal does not own tasks; its projects do.
- **2026-09-06: filed under the CQC.** Education is a **non-member function** of the Committee per CQC Charter § 2.8 — it delivers the education agenda the Committee adopts. That is what makes this the Committee's goal to hold even though education staff do not sit on it.
- **2026-09-06: [[Clinical Handbook]] stays where it is,** under [[Clinical Quality Framework]] only. It is the content this goal distributes, not a project of it — revisit if the orientation section splits off as tracked org work.
- **Every education initiative gets checked against short stay.** We train to the long stay; short-stay quality measures are poor and worse in the under-scheduled buildings, and heart failure gets said over and over precisely because people aren't comfortable managing it (2026-09-04, [[Clinical Quality Framework]]).

## Open questions

- **What the first real project under this goal is.** Candidates in view: rebuilding the Wednesday didactics around the active initiative, converting handbook chapters into the seminars, and new hire orientation. None named, none due.
- **Who owns education operationally.** There is a learning and development function and it attends the CQC as non-members; whether this goal directs it, partners with it, or is it, is unsettled.
- **What happens to the ACO's fourth Wednesday.** Dissolving it into specialty didactics is the argument; it is Sandra's slot to give up.
- **Does the iPhone app exist?** Will's assessment is that it isn't rocket science and the content is just text and links. That is an assessment, not a plan.
- **How education is measured.** A goal about quality education that cannot say whether anyone learned anything reproduces the CGSC — an agenda per meeting, no agenda in the sense of a mission. Every measure carries a target date or it doesn't count.

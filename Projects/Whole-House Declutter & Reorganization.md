## Context

Whole-house declutter and reorganization, attic to garage, addressing roughly 30 years of accumulation. The goal is to significantly minimize and build a lasting organization system — not a one-time purge.

**Three phases:**

1. **Purge** — room by room, not category-wide across the house. Each room's contents get sorted into Keep / Donate-Sell / Trash-Recycle / Undecided.
2. **Organize** — once a room's purge is done, build its navigation system: one home per category, zones by function, containers sized to the boundary (not the item), and an inbox bin for anything without a home yet.
3. **Sustain** — a seasonal re-pass each spring and fall, plus an ongoing one-in-one-out habit.

**Room sequence** (low-stakes to high-stakes, to build momentum before the hard rooms):

1. Kitchen/pantry
2. Bathrooms
3. Closets/clothes
4. Kids' rooms/toys
5. Home office/papers
6. Garage
7. Attic/basement — last, since this is usually where undecided/sentimental items have been quietly parked

**Pace:** one short session (~45-60 min) per week — kept deliberately small to avoid overwhelm. 37 sessions total, roughly Aug 2026 → Apr 2027, then into the Sustain phase. Full week-by-week plan in [[Whole-House Declutter - Schedule]].

## Decisions

- Purge is sequenced room by room, not category by category, so the whole house isn't torn apart at once.
- Room order is momentum-based (easiest rooms first, attic/basement last) rather than urgency- or size-based.
- Category-specific rules are locked in for kitchen/pantry, clothes, papers, toys, and sentimental items — see the [[Whole-House Declutter - Decision Rules Cheat Sheet|cheat sheet]] for the condensed version.
- Papers get a three-pile sort (shred / scan-then-shred / keep physical) feeding a `Household/Documents` scanned archive with consistent PDF naming, plus a fireproof box for true originals only.
- Toys use a rotation model: active toys out, a rotation set boxed and out of sight, anything untouched across two rotations gets donated. Best purged without young kids present; older kids are included in decisions.
- Sentimental items get a fixed-size bin per person, in any room — a full bin means something old goes before something new comes in.
- Progress is tracked by marking zones done in the room checklist and updating `last_touched` here as rooms complete.

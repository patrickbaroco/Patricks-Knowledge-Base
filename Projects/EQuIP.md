## Context

The insulin measure is the first concrete quality measure the new structure will run, and the W37 review named it, with the two-audit split, as [[Clinical Quality Framework]]'s first real content. Rucci has held it along with the governance packet since 2026-09-11.

It is the one piece of this work with a clock on it. The measure has to be announced early enough to allow two months of diabetes education before the first review cycle opens, which means the design questions cannot sit through another meeting.

They have now sat through two. Rucci sent her questions on 9/15. The 9/18 Quality Program Review was the scheduled place to answer them and the hour went to structure instead; she endorsed the plan there, and her questions were about seating it. Clinical Quality Part 2, Wednesday 2026-09-23 2:00–3:00pm on Teams, is where they land.

Her 2026-09-15 questions run to the design rather than the principle: the number of charts per reviewer, the pass/fail arithmetic against EQUiP, whether to add components while the reviewer is already in the chart, whether Jaime and Will should be in it, whether an APC might not have enough qualifying patients, the other three quarters, and how the roll-up to the attending handles psychiatric APCs. They are worked through in conversation, not tracked individually.

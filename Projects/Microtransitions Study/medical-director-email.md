**Subject:** Microtransitions study at [Facility]

Hi Dr. [Name],

[RDCOE] nominated [Facility] for a research study we've agreed to host, and I wanted to check with you before passing the facility's name to the research team.

Boston University's Microtransitions in Care Lab studies the small trips residents take out of the building for a clinic visit, dialysis, imaging, or lunch with family. Their finding is that about half of clinic visits send no note back to the facility, even though our staff are still expected to carry out whatever orders came out of the visit. They have built a one-page sign-out form that goes out with the resident and comes back to the nursing team, and they want to run it in twelve of our facilities for a year.

The ask of you is small. As medical director, you would invite the study team to present at QAPI, and they take it from there. They train the staff, about thirty minutes, do all of the chart review, and report progress and results back through QAPI. Nursing completes no additional documentation, and the form takes under ninety seconds. The study is grant funded, so it costs the facility nothing. Every building gets the intervention; only the start date is randomized.

If you are comfortable with it, let me know and I'll send the facility's information to Dr. Ben Canter, the principal investigator. If you have concerns about the building or the timing, I would rather hear them now. Agreeing does not commit the facility to anything. The study team presents at QAPI and leadership decides from there. If it would help to have me at the QAPI introduction, I'm glad to join.

I have attached their slides and a facility FAQ. Personally, I am excited about this one. It has long bothered me how little comes back with a resident from an outside appointment.

Patrick

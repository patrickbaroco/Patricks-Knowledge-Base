# QAPI request emails — Kendal, BRC, Heritage Hall (+ Rebecca Kline nudge)

All three study emails: cc Ben Canter <bcanter@bu.edu>. Attach `SNF Q&A Page.docx` (in To Share; same file sent to KD 10/23). No subject lines.

## Kendal at Lexington

To: Noelle Guill <nsguill@kalex.kendal.org>; Adam Feldbauer <afeldbauer@kalex.kendal.org>
Cc: bcanter@bu.edu

Hi Noelle and Adam,

Would you be willing to pull together a short ad hoc QAPI so a research team from Boston University can introduce a study? I'd like Kendal to be one of the first three buildings in it. They need about 15 minutes, can join by Teams or Zoom, and are generally free Tuesday through Thursday. Ben Canter, the principal investigator, is copied here so you can find a time with him directly.

The study is about what the lab calls microtransitions: the small trips residents take out of the building for a clinic visit, dialysis, imaging, or lunch with family. Their finding is that about half of clinic visits send no note back to the facility, even though our nurses are still expected to carry out whatever orders came out of the visit. They've built a one-page sign-out form that goes out with the resident and comes back to the nursing team on return.

The ask of the building is small. Whoever escorts a resident out fills in the form, which takes about 90 seconds. Nursing does no extra documentation. The study team does the staff training (about 30 minutes) and all the chart review, and reports progress back through QAPI. It's grant funded, so it costs Kendal nothing. Every building gets the intervention; only the start date is randomized.

Hearing them out doesn't commit Kendal to anything, and you can decide after the meeting. I've attached their FAQ.

Personally, I'm excited about this one. It has long bothered me how little comes back with a resident from an outside appointment.

Patrick

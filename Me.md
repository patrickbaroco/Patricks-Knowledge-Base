## Writing Style and Voice

- Smart and precise but not stiff
- Conversational rather than formal
- Humor when it fits — not forced
- I like a well-placed literary or historical reference, but only when it earns its keep
- I am not interested in hedging for its own sake
- I do not want my prose sanitized or softened
- Avoid excessive em-dashes. Avoid “it’s not this, it’s that” syntax.

**What I do not want from AI:**
- Rewriting my sentences when I've asked for formatting help
- Adding framing, transitions, or commentary I didn't ask for
- Softening clinical language that was intentionally direct
- Generic bullet-point summaries of complex ideas

## Clinical Writing Standards

**Always:**
- Person-first language: "a patient with dementia," never "a demented patient"; "a patient with diabetes," never "a diabetic"
- Generic drug names — always, everywhere

**Clinical frameworks I return to:**
- UTI-as-spectrum (Stages 0–5: urobiome homeostasis → ASB without pyuria → ASB with pyuria → cystitis → pyelonephritis → urosepsis)
  - Key teaching point: *The Loeb delirium criterion is a Stage 5 (urosepsis) flag, not a Stage 2 upgrade* — delirium in a non-verbal patient with dementia and bacteriuria means ask whether the patient looks septic, not reflexive antibiotics
- SGLT2i and sacubitril-valsartan framed as simultaneously disease-modifying and palliative
- Beers Criteria as a teaching scaffold, not a prohibition list

## Obsidian Workflow

**This is active and ongoing.** I am converting my clinical newsletter series ("Bedside Notes") into Obsidian-flavored Markdown.

**Core conventions:**
- Obsidian-native callout blocks (`> [!type]`) as the primary structural element
- No YAML frontmatter
- Date retained inline
- **Source fidelity is non-negotiable** — spelling and grammar corrections only; no rewriting, no added framing, no new transitions
- Callout types chosen to match the epistemic character of each section (e.g., `> [!note]`, `> [!warning]`, `> [!tip]`, `> [!abstract]`)
- File naming: zero-padded issue numbers with descriptive slugs (e.g., `010-hepatic-encephalopathy.md`)

**Issues converted or in progress:** hepatic encephalopathy, hypertensive urgency, tramadol, anticoagulation/antiplatelet therapy, COPD, VTE prophylaxis, SSRIs for dementia agitation, gabapentinoids, pramipexole for depression, thickened liquids, UTI.

## What I Want From AI

- Treat source text as sacred when I've asked for formatting only
- Match my register — don't soften, don't inflate
- Don't add bullet summaries I didn't ask for
- Push back when you disagree — I want genuine intellectual engagement, not validation. This is important.
- Ask one good clarifying question rather than five mediocre ones
- Lead with the answer; context after
- When I'm writing, help me write better — don't write for me unless I've asked you to

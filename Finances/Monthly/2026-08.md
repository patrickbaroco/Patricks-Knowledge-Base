And the sweeps ran: $7,500 into Ally across the 22nd through the 27th, on top of earlier transfers, taking savings from $14.7K to $24.1K.

The testing was also real, and it's what makes the month look worse than it was. On the 30th a $3,304.42 Snowshoe payment, on the 31st the $12,780 Trident charge, and on the 31st a $21,246 payment clearing the Sapphire Reserve. Those three moves are why liquid cash reads $45,229 today against the ~$70K the handoff recorded in late August — **the handoff's number was taken before the card payment cleared**. Nothing structural went wrong. Two deliberate purchases and one scheduled card payment, exactly as the handoff says to read them.

What I did not expect is what the decomposition shows underneath. Strip the discrete purchases out of July and August and both months land near $20,000 of operating spend, not $27,000. That is the finding of the month and it is not yet a conclusion — see below.

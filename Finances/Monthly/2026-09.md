## Monthly rollup

September was the month the system changed shape twice. On the 20th savings became one number against one target; on the 26th that target gave way to the two-fund test, a sinking fund that should run close to zero at the June trough and a surplus fund that should keep growing. The money itself was quiet. Income was $30,077 against $27,301 of spending, with no Sapphire Reserve payment in the month because the $21,246 payment on 31 August covered it. The sweep landed in full, $3,600 across two transfers after the first came in $600 short. MES ran well above its floor at $7,703, and USAA sent $13,000 across to Ally Spending in two ad hoc transfers. The one discrete item was Snowtracks, $2,050.92 for Marcus's ski season, which belongs to the sinking fund.

The baseline got its third month. On the same basis as July and August, September's operating spend is $20,747, the lowest of the three, in the first month to carry a full EV9 payment. Allison's card was the complication: it had never been in the history. Her full year is now in Era, and with it the three months read $21,961, $22,793 and $21,785, averaging $22,180. The November quarterly decides what to do with that.

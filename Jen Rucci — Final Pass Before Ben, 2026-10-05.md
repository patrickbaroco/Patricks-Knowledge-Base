## The seat map reads as a hierarchy

The primary care and psychiatry committees are drawn beneath the Quality Committee, and the wording ("hands down," "send down," "feed Quality") reads as subordination. That is an org-chart reading, and people will take it as inferiority. The relationship is lateral: each department sets the standards for its own discipline, the Quality Committee operationalizes them, and priorities can start on either side (the OCH or education members of Quality may notice a problem and ask primary care to write a standard). The circular diagram is closer, and still sits underneath.

**Fix: lateralize everything.** Departments beside the Quality Committee as equals, arrows back and forth, wording to match. Rucci suggested the departments might go on the left, since they have nothing to do with peer review. I do not think the charters need changing; they already state each department's responsibility.

## EQuIP

**Diabetes overtreatment.** I presented it: any resident with A1c below 7% on insulin or a sulfonylurea, with de-escalation or a documented reason not to. Barb says it is easy to pull from A1c and the med list. Rucci: "I like that."

Inflammatory bowel disease encompasses Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, and microscopic colitis — chronic immune-mediated intestinal inflammation whose workup begins by excluding infection.

## Subtypes and Distribution
- **Crohn's disease** — transmural inflammation, **skip lesions**, granulomas, anywhere from mouth to anus with a predilection for the **terminal ileum**; **rectal sparing** is characteristic. **Ileal involvement with rectal sparing in a young patient favored Crohn's** on the RLR case ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).
- **Ulcerative colitis** — continuous mucosal inflammation extending proximally **from the rectum**, limited to the colon.
- **Microscopic colitis** (collagenous and lymphocytic) — chronic **watery diarrhea with a normal-appearing mucosa**; diagnosis rests on biopsy, and it skews to older patients.
- Immune-mediated mimics such as **Behçet's** disease can reproduce ileal colitis with deep ulcers and neutrophilic infiltrate and must stay on the list ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]], see [[Behçet's Disease]]).

## Systemic and Extraintestinal Manifestations
- Chronic inflammation produces **anemia (normocytic or iron-deficiency), thrombocytosis, and elevated inflammatory markers** — all present in the RLR patient ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).
- **Extraintestinal manifestations** span joints (peripheral and axial arthritis), skin (erythema nodosum, pyoderma gangrenosum), eyes (uveitis, episcleritis), and hepatobiliary tract (primary sclerosing cholangitis, more with UC).
- Some manifestations track disease activity (peripheral arthritis, erythema nodosum) while others run independently (axial arthritis, PSC).

## Workup — Exclude Infection First
The disciplined rule is to **rule out infection before immunosuppressing**, because infection can both mimic and **trigger a flare** ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).

- **Stool infectious testing** — *C. difficile* toxin/PCR, bacterial cultures, and **multiplex/NAAT (e.g., BioFire) PCR** ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).
- **Inflammatory markers** — **ESR/CRP** for systemic activity ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).
- **Stool inflammatory markers** — **[[Fecal Calprotectin]]** and **[[Stool Lactoferrin]]** distinguish inflammatory from functional diarrhea; interpret by pretest probability ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).
- **Endoscopy (ileocolonoscopy) with biopsy** — the diagnostic anchor for pattern, depth, and histology; MR/CT enterography maps small bowel and complications.
- Remember **ileum-favoring infections** (Yersinia, TB) as caveats when the ileum is inflamed ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).

## Treatment Overview
- **Induction** — corticosteroids for acute flares (systemic, or budesonide for ileocecal/right-sided disease); 5-aminosalicylates for mild ulcerative colitis.
- **Maintenance / steroid-sparing** — thiopurines (azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine), methotrexate, and **biologics/small molecules** (anti-TNF, anti-integrin, anti-IL-12/23, JAK inhibitors) tailored to subtype and severity.
- **Surgery** — curative colectomy for ulcerative colitis; in Crohn's, resection is reserved for strictures, fistulae, or refractory disease and is not curative.
- Ongoing needs include **health-maintenance** (vaccination before immunosuppression, colorectal cancer surveillance in longstanding colitis).

## Clinical-Reasoning Notes
- IBD is often the correct **base-rate track** in a young patient with chronic relapsing colitis — but stay **removable** if a soft clue (pathergy, undisclosed ulcers) reframes the case toward a mimic ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]], see [[Diagnostic Momentum]]).
- Keep **"autoimmune" broader than "rheumatologic"** so immune-mediated GI disease does not fall off the checklist ([[RLR 063 - Diarrhea and Behçet's Disease|RLR 063]]).

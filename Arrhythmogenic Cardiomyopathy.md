Evergreen concept note. Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy is a functional grouping of myocardial diseases that destroy the conduction system and generate arrhythmia disproportionately early relative to pump failure. RLR / Clinical Problem Solvers teaching points are cited per episode; general clinical detail is scaffolding.

## The Unifying Idea
Any cardiomyopathy can eventually wreck conduction, but a distinct subset does so **out of proportion to pump dysfunction** — presenting with advanced [[AV Block]] or ventricular tachycardia while the ejection fraction is still preserved. That pattern is the clue ([[RLR 060 - Transient Loss of Consciousness and Cardiac Sarcoidosis|RLR 060]]).

- **Troponin + advanced conduction disease + preserved ventricular function** should call this family to mind ([[RLR 060 - Transient Loss of Consciousness and Cardiac Sarcoidosis|RLR 060]]).
- Mechanistically, these diseases lay down **patchy infiltration or scar** in the conduction tissue and myocardium before global contractile failure sets in.

## Causes
- **Cardiac sarcoidosis** — non-caseating granulomas favoring the basal septum/conduction system; patchy delayed gadolinium enhancement and FDG-PET uptake. See [[Cardiac Sarcoidosis]] ([[RLR 060 - Transient Loss of Consciousness and Cardiac Sarcoidosis|RLR 060]]).
- **Cardiac amyloidosis** — infiltrative (ATTR, AL) with low-voltage/pseudo-infarct EKG, conduction disease, and increased wall thickness; a common late-life infiltrative cause.
- **Genetic / desmosomal disease** — **ARVC** (desmosomal mutations; fibrofatty replacement of the RV, VT, epsilon waves), **HCM** (sarcomeric; arrhythmia and conduction disease), and **lamin A/C (LMNA) cardiomyopathy**, notorious for early conduction disease and high sudden-death risk ([[RLR 060 - Transient Loss of Consciousness and Cardiac Sarcoidosis|RLR 060]]).
- Overlap: infiltrative and genetic processes can coexist, and family history/genetic testing refines the specific etiology.

## Why It Matters
- **Diagnostic:** conduction disease with a preserved pump is a pointer, not an endpoint — it should trigger cardiac MRI (delayed enhancement), and, when sarcoid is possible, PET and tissue.
- **Prognostic/therapeutic:** these substrates carry disproportionate **sudden-death risk**, so device decisions (often favoring an **ICD** rather than a pacemaker alone) and, where relevant, disease-specific therapy (steroids for sarcoid, tafamidis for ATTR amyloid, genotype-guided care for LMNA) hinge on identifying the cause.

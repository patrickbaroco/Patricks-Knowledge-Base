Evergreen concept note. Gorham-Stout (vanishing/disappearing bone) disease. Teaching points cited per episode.

## The entity

Gorham-Stout disease is a rare, idiopathic **massive osteolysis** in which bone is progressively replaced by proliferating **vascular and lymphatic** channels — "vanishing" or "disappearing" bone. It has a predilection for the skull and femur, no known risk factors, and a **biopsy-clinched** diagnosis (near-complete replacement of bone by lymphovascular tissue is characteristic). Treatment is speculative — bisphosphonates are used, of unproven efficacy (also anti-angiogenics/sirolimus, radiation in some reports). It is a *benign* primary bone disease. ([[RLR 031 - Headache and Gorham-Stout Disease|RLR 031]])

## Reasoning points

- **Reachable by category even when the specific entity is unknown.** The [[Lytic Bone Lesion|lytic-lesion schema]] localizes it to "solitary benign primary bone disease" before the name is known — the episode's thesis that you can reason to a category without having the diagnosis scripted. ([[RLR 031 - Headache and Gorham-Stout Disease|RLR 031]])
- **A benign lesion can be FDG-avid** — the dense lymphovascular proliferation takes up glucose, so PET avidity here misled toward malignancy in the [[Cognitive Autopsy|cognitive autopsy]]. ([[RLR 031 - Headache and Gorham-Stout Disease|RLR 031]])

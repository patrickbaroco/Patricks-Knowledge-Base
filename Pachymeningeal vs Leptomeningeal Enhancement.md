A radiographic distinction on contrast MRI — dura (pachymeninges) versus arachnoid-and-pia (leptomeninges) — that carries two different differentials.

## The Orange-Peel Model
A useful mental picture: the **dura is the peel**, thick and following the inner table smoothly; the **leptomeninges (arachnoid + pia) are the wispy layer** hugging the brain surface and dipping into the sulci and around segments/roots ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]).

- **Pattern recognition:** pachymeningeal enhancement is **thick, continuous, and does not enter the sulci**; leptomeningeal enhancement is **thin and follows the pial surface into sulci, cisterns, cranial nerves, and nerve roots** ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]).
- **Roots are leptomeningeal.** Spinal nerve roots are wrapped in leptomeninges, so **root enhancement is a leptomeningeal sign** ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]).

## Pachymeningeal (Dural) Differential
Thick, non-sulcal dural enhancement ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]):

- **Smooth/diffuse:** **intracranial hypotension / CSF leak** (low-pressure headache) and **dural venous sinus thrombosis** are the marquee non-neoplastic causes.
- **Lumpy/nodular:** **dural metastases**, **neurosarcoidosis**, and **rheumatoid or vasculitic (hypertrophic) pachymeningitis**; IgG4-related disease and infectious (e.g., TB) pachymeningitis also belong here.

## Leptomeningeal (Arachnoid/Pia) Differential
Thin, sulcal/cisternal/root-following enhancement ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]):

- **Infectious/inflammatory meningitis** (bacterial, fungal, TB, viral).
- **Neurosarcoidosis** (can do either compartment).
- **Carcinomatous meningitis / leptomeningeal metastases** — in a cancer patient, diffuse leptomeningeal enhancement over brain, cord, and roots is [[Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|leptomeningeal carcinomatosis]] until proven otherwise ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]).

## Reading It as an Interpretation
- **The compartment narrows the workup**, not the diagnosis alone — pair the pattern with the tempo, systemic clues, and CSF.
- **Enhancement can be absent** in thin leptomeningeal disease; a normal MRI does not exclude it, just as PET may be falsely negative (see [[Test Interpretation]]) ([[RLR 059 - Weakness and Numbness and Leptomeningeal Carcinomatosis|RLR 059]]).

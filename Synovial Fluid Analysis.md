Synovial fluid is read like CSF or pleural fluid — a small structured panel whose results are interpreted together, in order, and never as a single clinching number.

## Framework — Read It Like CSF or Pleural Fluid
- **Same mental model as other body-fluid taps** ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]): inspect gross appearance, then cell count and differential, then crystals, then Gram stain/culture (± PCR). Each result is contextual.
- **No single cell count clinches a diagnosis** ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]) and **multiple processes coexist** — infection can trigger a crystal flare, so a positive crystal does not exclude sepsis.

## Gross Appearance
- Clear/straw = non-inflammatory; cloudy/turbid = inflammatory or septic (increasing cellularity/protein); bloody = hemorrhagic effusion — raising trauma, and, recalling anticoagulation, a coagulopathy ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).

## WBC Ranges (overlapping, not absolute)
- **Non-inflammatory:** < 2,000/µL (osteoarthritis, trauma).
- **Inflammatory:** ~2,000–100,000/µL (crystal disease, autoimmune) ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).
- **Septic:** frequently > 50,000/µL, and often much higher, but the ranges **overlap** — no number is diagnostic ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).
- **% PMN:** a neutrophil predominance > 75% leans bacterial ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).

## Crystals, Gram Stain, and Culture
- **Crystals** — needle-shaped, negatively birefringent = monosodium urate ([[Gout]]); rhomboid, weakly positively birefringent = calcium pyrophosphate ([[CPPD]]). Crystals can be falsely negative (especially CPPD) and are only *active* when intracellular — see [[Crystals in Macrophages]].
- **Gram stain** — highly specific but only ~50% sensitive; a positive stain ends the conversation as [[Septic Arthritis|septic]] ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).
- **Culture** — ~80% sensitive (roughly 20% falsely negative) ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]).

## Read-Order and Likelihood-Ratio Thresholds
- **Read in sequence: Gram stain → crystals → cell count LAST** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]). A positive Gram stain settles it; crystals come next; the cell count is then interpreted *in that context* as a probability-of-sepsis estimator, not a standalone answer.
- **WBC likelihood-ratio thresholds** ([[RLR 062 - Oligoarthritis and Polyarticular Gout Flare|RLR 062]]): the probability of sepsis rises meaningfully **past ~50,000** and skyrockets **above ~100,000**, per the JAMA Rational Clinical Examination "Does this adult patient have septic arthritis?" A value in between (e.g., 70,000) sits in a genuinely ambiguous zone where the decision becomes a [[Bayesian Reasoning|risk-tolerance]] judgment.

## Pitfalls
- **Adjacent, not intra-articular, inflammation yields bland fluid** — the [[Spillover Phenomenon]] / brain-abscess-LP principle ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]): a quiet tap next to a raging structure is deceptively reassuring.
- **A cell count discordant with clinical severity should trigger reframing** via the [[Law of Proportionality]] ([[RLR 061 - Knee Swelling and Calcific Tendinitis|RLR 061]]).

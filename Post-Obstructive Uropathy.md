Post-obstructive uropathy and the post-obstructive diuresis that follows relief.

## Two mechanisms of the post-obstructive diuresis

- **A solute washout and a concentrating defect — and they are not the same.** After an obstruction is relieved, the classic diuresis is an **osmotic/solute washout** (accumulated urea and sodium dragging water out). But obstruction also injures the distal nephron: it **downregulates [[Aquaporin|aquaporin]] channel expression** at the collecting duct, producing a **nephrogenic [[Diabetes Insipidus|diabetes insipidus]]** in which even released ADH has nothing to act on. The very dilute urine (specific gravity 1.001) is the tell that it is *more* than a solute washout. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])
- **A distal (hyperkalemic) RTA lives next door.** The same distal-nephron injury can produce a distal/hyperkalemic renal tubular acidosis alongside the concentrating defect. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])

## The reasoning traps

- **You need not have AKI for post-obstructive uropathy to cause nephrogenic DI.** The concentrating defect can follow obstruction even without a big creatinine rise, so its absence does not exclude the mechanism. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])
- **Matching the output with IV fluids sustains the diuresis.** Running fluids to keep pace with a post-obstructive diuresis iatrogenically feeds a solute diuresis; stopping fluids and applying the [[Return to the Bedside|test of time]] lets the urine concentrate and confirms the diagnosis. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])
- **Missing the script lets an incidental scan mislead.** Without the aquaporin-downregulation mechanism in mind, a dilute urine "for a solute diuresis" is confusing and a [[Pituitary Macroadenoma|pituitary incidentaloma]] can pull toward central DI — a [[Cognitive Autopsy|cognitive-autopsy]] lesson in interpreting unfamiliar data. ([[RLR 034 - Polyuria and Post-Obstructive Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus|RLR 034]])

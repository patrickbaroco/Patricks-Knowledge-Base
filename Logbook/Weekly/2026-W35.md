## Weekly Rollup

The week the peer review program went from an approved intention to a complete draft package — and the week the regional CMO role actually ended.

What it did produce was a nine-minute admin pass at 9:25am from the iPad — six items, gone — and two design decisions that show up dated in the project brief: the primary care and psych committees are not peer review bodies and won't be chartered as such, and the CQC sits outside the PSES.

**Wednesday** was BRC rounds and then the design day, and it was the most consequential day of the week. Single-entity corporate structure confirmed across all five states. CGC composition resolved. The board framing switched from patient safety to **exit diligence**. The standards architecture settled — minimum expectation versus improvement target, the economic-credentialing rule rewritten, *"selection is not a finding"* added after stress-testing an antipsychotic-in-dementia scenario, and *"the standard of care is not displaced."* Cadence decided. The records custodian made a chartered position. And the answer that unblocked the rest: no privileges exist today, and the executive team supports creating them.

**Thursday** was office hours and the board resolution — v0.1, ten sections, establishing the Council and delegating professional review authority to it. Fourteen minutes before it, a pass at the Claude voice instructions.

**Friday** was six documents and a handoff. The objections brief expanded to cover the whole program with plain-language answers under each objection; the coordinator duties tied line-by-line to charter citations; the three charters drafted in a four-minute window at 5:12–5:16pm; the fair hearing procedure and a full project-brief rewrite at 10:03pm. And at 5:53pm, between the charters and the evening block, **the transfer note went out to the team**: Dr. Surah Grumet relieves Patrick as regional CMO effective Monday 8/31. The transition announced on 8/12 is now executed.

**Saturday** closed the package at 12:48am with the executive summary — the version leadership and the board can actually read — and then belonged entirely to Vincent. The Augusta Invitational cross country meet in the morning, where both boys ran great times and **Vincent broke six minutes in the mile on his own birthday**, then maki night at home with Mom and Dad over. The sushi was wonderful.

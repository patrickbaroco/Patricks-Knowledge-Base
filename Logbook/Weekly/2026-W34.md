Thursday was the payoff. Office hours in the morning, notes worked through between meetings, and then the Jeff Wells meeting — which went about as well as it could have. He is **eager to expand the peer review process and eager to be a partner in it**, which is close to the best possible starting position, since the legal architecture is exactly what the program is gated on and Jeff is the person who can unlock it. Physician assignments settled in a separate meeting: Rucci and Dr. Surah Grumet, the new regional CMO, will be the ones having those conversations with the docs. Will Sampson, head of Learning and Development, opened the education-initiatives thread, to be picked up again in a week or two. ACO advisory committee in the evening, nothing new out of it.

Friday closed the transition's first real loop: notes with Lindsey finished, and **all of the initial meetings set up — Jacob, Cortney, and Sandeep**. That converts the new role from an intention into a calendar. Midday, peer review became a proper project note — the first rebuild out of the Chair of Clinical Excellence structure parked back on 8/16 — with one gating task on it, and two badly drifted project notes got reconciled in the same pass. The afternoon went to a PSO primer and a five-state privilege comparison. The EV9 GT was picked up, correctly this time.

The weekend was personal infrastructure and a reckoning. Saturday: a new email address on Google Workspace, four miles on the Waynesboro greenway, a current-state assessment of what peer review already looks like at Eventus, and the start of a voice-samples note for teaching Claude to draft in his own voice. Sunday brought a morning walk, more voice work, meals planned and prepped for the week — and the week's real decision point, covered below.

**The pattern worth naming this week:** for four days Todoist and the actual work were completely decoupled. Not one task was completed between Monday evening and Friday, while Tuesday through Thursday were three of the strongest days of the month. Then on Sunday afternoon the whole thing cleared in a single pass — twelve items, every recurring admin sign-off and five stale meal-prep tasks. That is now the second consecutive week of **batch reconciliation** rather than daily check-off. Combined with the Friday 8/14 one-pass clear, this looks like the working pattern rather than a lapse, and is worth designing the system around instead of correcting.

## Weekly Rollup

> [!note] Rollup written 2026-10-04
> From the seven dailies and Outlook. Friday through Sunday were backfilled without a mail sweep at the time; Sent Items for those days was read for this rollup.

The week the committees reached the people who would sit on them, and the two oldest carries did not move.

**Monday put numbers in front of a committee.** I chaired the CGSC on antipsychotic use in dementia: rules and rates, rates concentrated in about fifty buildings, the rate climbing with dementia density. The framing went unchallenged and the objections went to the denominator and to why ALF runs so far above SNF, which is the right place for them. Rucci closed with *"we've made no progress, and it's almost October."* In the hour after, she told me all three 2026 company goals, antipsychotics, anxiolytics and ALF transitions of care, have been missed, and took apart the largest outlier on my list in five minutes: Durham Ridge is a psychiatric building whose dementia diagnoses exist to keep it clear of IMD status. That is the clearest argument I have for never attributing a rate to a clinician. The Virginia, North Carolina and Kentucky lists were back by Wednesday; Valerie's took a second asking, and the unclear ask was mine. The insulin measure grew into [[EQuIP]].

**Tuesday the charters were rebuilt around Rucci and sent the same day.** Referrals now go through me and the department chair for the clinician's service line, APCs review APCs, an agreed education plan closes a matter, and the evening stress test added a concern flag for the chart that meets the standard and still records care nobody would defend. The packet went at 5:51pm: *"The CEO authorization here is just an example, obviously. I just wanted to make the point that this could be very simple."* At 10pm I gave Jennie's psychiatrists the plainest version yet of rates and rules: a facility rate is a signal to look, a rule is a standard the chart meets or does not, and a supervising physician who finds the standard met can still say *"Good job, you met the standard. But let me share a few thoughts about quetiapine."* Grumet's plan to hand QAPI scheduling to Missy Artis went to Rucci with my objection: at my well-run facilities a solution in search of a problem, at the less well-run ones a problem-generator.

**Dr. Tompkins resigned.** His letter came Monday at 7:22pm, outstanding notes completed; I answered Tuesday morning. I hate to see him leave, though I'm not surprised, and I'm very glad it is no longer my problem. It's Surah's. See [[2026-09-29-Tu]].

**Wednesday and Thursday the structure went out and came back well received, twice.** At the ANR QAPI, with Stephen Carl from Avardis in the room, I announced the weekend coverage plan, and both jobs went live in UKG and Indeed the same day. Colvin read the governance packet properly, called it a breath of fresh air, and brought it two cases: a physician candidate with a prior license suspension, and APCs with repeat open notes that HR has not moved. Thursday the Regional CMO Sync heard the governance plan and received it well, and Dirk followed at 3pm with a name for the distinguished physician seat, Dr. Sarah Cunningham. Will agreed to an education plan, the piece the insulin measure needs before it launches. Rucci's comments on the charters, due Wednesday, did not arrive, and I have heard nothing about her Thursday briefing of Ben.

**Friday gave the diabetes measure a second leg and moved the Fidelity chain.** Facilities with nobody on corrective insulin pass automatically, so I asked Barb Pereira for an overtreatment component: A1c below 7.0% on any insulin or sulfonylurea, met by acknowledgment plus a change or a stated reason to continue. The weekend coverage email went to Ashley Winkle and Alyssa; Ashley wants to focus on recruitment and to set time with Alyssa and me on the 2567. The charters went to Patel. The identity call to Fidelity was made and Allison's Self-Employed 401(k) and deferral election were done, the step that had gated the whole chain.

**The weekend was systems, the handbook and family.** Saturday the week's spending number read red and was corrected the same day, and the finance review system was rebuilt around three long-horizon decisions. Sunday the drainfield got its second glyphosate pass, the governance charters were revised so most audits end in a review report and an offer of help, and the handbook had its biggest day in months: Diabetes in LTC rewritten at twice its length, three quick references, and all nine regulatory and orientation chapters from stub to draft (see [[Clinical Handbook]]). Mom and Dad came for dinner and brought a table they made for Vincent's aquarium.

**What did not move.** The KD warfarin reply, the week's only p1, is a week old, and KD's self-report decision waits on it. The letters to the four microtransitions medical directors, drafted 9/23, are still unsent with the Saber call on Tuesday. Most of Saturday's errands carried, as did both finance reviews.

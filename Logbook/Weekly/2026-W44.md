## Weekly Rollup

The week belonged to Dr. Craig Mitchell. He had resigned, and on Wednesday I went through his concerns with him one by one. Eventus had already recognized every one of them and was making progress on all of them, which left his ask: a $20,000 retention bonus. Cary heard it on the phone Thursday afternoon and took it to Ben, who approved it by Friday morning, paid out over 24 months and with Dr. Grumet's approval. Four structures were on his desk by 10:00, and Mitchell agreed to the shape of it. In the afternoon Rucci asked that Dr. Grumet take it over. I disagreed in writing, put Dr. Grumet on the thread anyway, prepared Mitchell for the hand-off and talked it through with her. By 4:17 Cary had made it a joint effort. By evening Mitchell was agreeable to a new contract, Rucci was calling the abrupt transition a lesson learned, and Ben was asking how to help close it.

The late-notes policy moved under the same pressure. Notes signed after two business days are paid at 50%, and Mitchell's exit conversation had cited the policy. On Wednesday Steve Colvin proposed dropping the financial penalty for an EQuIP multiplier on notes closed within 48 hours, which would move enforcement from HR to clinical governance. My own 10/19 proposal keeps the cut and adds a tiered on-time bonus. Cary and I discussed both on Thursday's call.

All three QAPIs happened: Shenandoah and ANR on Tuesday, KD on Thursday. Before KD's I caught up with Ashley Winkle, working to keep that relationship good after KD declined the microtransitions study.

Clinical Governance settled two questions. Monday evening's four linked charter revisions agreed who votes and who screens: the CQC Chair votes except on a referral, Department Chairs withdraw from referrals in their own service line, and a PRC agreed resolution is proposed only on the Review Panel's concurrence. Tuesday night *Committee Time and Cost* answered Rucci's cost question at about 46–48 budgeted hours a month across both committees, roughly $44,000–47,000 a year, half the full-admin-day convention. She chose the slightly costlier model for the physician opportunity. Mary Linton Kops and Kat McDougal took CQC seats on Wednesday.

Thursday, the one desk day, went to other clinicians' questions: capacity certification under Virginia law, a probable checkpoint-inhibitor rash, an effusion that did not need ceftriaxone and metronidazole. Two came through the pharmacist inbox, and that became a proposal to Rucci under [[Clinical Questions Pathway]]. Friday I returned Althea Davis's diabetes toolkit a week early, and the morning review cleared most of what had been carried since September: committee and distinguished-physician nominations, the AP targets, the OCH deck, Picasso MD's referrals with Will.

The Kendal and BRC email for the microtransitions study, a `top3` most of the week, did not move. HCC Module 10 passed its deadline. The personal side was thin: Eat Your Books cancelled, Lincoln's *Think Anew* saved to Quotes.

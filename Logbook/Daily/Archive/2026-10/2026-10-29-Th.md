**Capacity certification, 7:41am**, on the UDA training-video thread (Rucci, Patel, Wolfram, Grumet, Hall, Clements, Juschka). Virginia has no legal way to say a person lacks capacity and always will. A physician's capacity determination answers a specific medical question and can change within a day; a person can have capacity to choose DNR without having it for full code. On finances, a physician's determination only activates a financial POA. Only a court removes decisional authority. If the facility's generalized PCC capacity questions must be answered, our own record should say what we mean by capacity.

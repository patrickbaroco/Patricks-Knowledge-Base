The meadow moved further today than in the two months before it. Detail is in [[Meadow Conversion]], [[Meadow Timeline]] and [[Materials Quantities for Meadow Project]]; the short version is that it stopped being one project:

- **Walked and measured** the front zone (1,000 m²) and the drainfield (1,067 m² after the orchard took the top of it). Total drops 0.897 → 0.85 → **0.74 acres** across the day.
- **Expanded the orchard** ~6× and settled its floor: bare rings under each tree, existing grass mowed to 3–5 in. between, optional clover frost-seeded Feb 2027. Vole prevention drove it, and it happens to make the [[Cherry Tree]] leaf-spot raking practical — noted in that note too.
- **Re-scoped the whole thing.** The gully and front are not meadow ground; they're woodland — infill trees plus a no-mow fine fescue floor. Glyphosate drops from 0.74 to 0.26 acres, the Roundstone Mix 181 order is cancelled, and the front lawn never goes brown. That last one is the real win: the 18-month ugly phase no longer touches the zone visible from the road.
- **Rescheduled the knockdown** — Application 1 Sept 12–13, Application 2 Oct 3–4. September is not too late; frost is the binding constraint, not the calendar.
- **Bee forage review**, prompted by actually accounting for the hives: the Ernst mix is 76% grass, strong in late summer and fall, essentially nothing in spring. Orchard bloom and the clover cover that gap. The hives get nothing from the meadow until 2028.
- Left open: the front-zone sun-hour count, which is a go/no-go for fescue, and the A/B/C decision on over-seed versus kill-and-reseed.

**Work**
**Five PRC charter revisions were drafted in one session, 7:26–8:49am** — the largest single day of charter work since September. All five came out of one live matter: general counsel had asked for a quality-of-care review of a family complaint, with no clinical body to send the clinical questions to.

- **§ 15.11.1, notice to counsel** (open item 53). Counsel gets one answer with a fixed meaning — whether the matter presents a circumstance requiring counsel's attention — and the care itself goes to peer review. §§ 4.5 and 12.1 conformed, with a recusal for anyone who reviewed the care at counsel's request.
- **§§ 4.4.4 and 4.5.2, the safety question at screening** (item 54). Screeners now ask at every screening whether patients need protecting while the matter runs; Management, as referrer, hears only that no action is required or that a restriction is in place. Raised because the clinician's supervisor had followed up on the same complaint.
- **§ 9.9, notice to Management of an interim restriction** (item 55), closing an Article IX gap flagged earlier the same morning: Management schedules the Practitioner, so a restriction it did not refer still has to reach it — terms only, on the day it is imposed.
- **§ 6.8, the sample** (item 56). Every opened review looks at [five] other recent patients in the same clinical area, identified by the Coordinator, disclosed once in the opening notice — no second notice when scope widens, because two notices are high-stress for a clinician. Narrowed later in the session so the ten-business-day response applies only to a new finding on sampled care.
- **§ 8.5.2, teaching points** (item 57). Every teaching point is given, however minor, and labelled apart from any finding: the prime mission is to improve quality, and every clinician is assumed to want that.

Counsel questions were added for four of the five — privilege on the fixed answer, recusal after a review for counsel, negligent-supervision exposure on the answer to Management, and whether teaching points could later show the Entity knew of a knowledge gap. **The companion documents are not conformed** to any of the five, and the PRC still awaits Ben's approval.

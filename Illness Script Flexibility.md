Evergreen concept note. A facet of the [[Illness Script]] hub. Source episodes are cited per example.

## Definition

Illness-script flexibility is the willingness to widen a script's boundaries — its severity, tempo, or histology — so that a textbook host presenting atypically is not under-weighted. A rigid script ("this organism is indolent"; "this disease isn't granulomatous") excludes the right diagnosis on a technicality; flexibility stretches the script to fit the host and the histology in front of you.

## How it operates

- **Rigidity is a miss mechanism.** A script held too narrowly rejects a correct diagnosis because one feature (severity, chronicity, histology) falls outside the remembered version.
- **Let the host stretch the script.** The right predisposing context (chronic steroids, a specific exposure) should widen a script's plausible severity and presentation.
- **Flexibility is bounded, not infinite.** It widens a script to fit strong host/histology evidence — not a license to force any diagnosis onto any patient.

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **Widening the granuloma script.** Non-caseating granulomas narrow to infection, sarcoidosis, and lymphoma; with infection excluded and lymphoma's features too strong to miss, sarcoid wins — an exercise in flexing the script. ([[RLR 011 - Fever and Chronic Consolidation|RLR 011]])
- **Rigidity as the named miss.** Nocardia was scripted as indolent and not scripted as granulomatous at all, so a textbook host (chronic prednisone, gardener) with the right histology was under-weighted — the fix is to widen the script's severity and histology. ([[RLR 017 - Back Pain and Disseminated Nocardiosis|RLR 017]])

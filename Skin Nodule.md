A schema for cutaneous nodules.

## The schema: deep, or infiltrating from afar

Nodules are **deep-space** lesions, so they mean one of two things — something deep *within* the skin, or something that has *infiltrated* the skin from elsewhere:

- **Primary deep dermatoses** — [[Panniculitis|panniculitis]] (disease of the fat, very deep) or a [[Sweet Syndrome|neutrophilic dermatosis]] (Sweet syndrome the classic).
- **Infiltration from afar** — granulomatous disease ([[Cutaneous Tuberculosis|nodular TB]], sarcoid) or a metastatic tumor: *liquid* ([[Leukemia Cutis|leukemia cutis]]) or *solid*. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])

## Reasoning points

- **Nodules imply chronicity** (they don't appear overnight) — unless they are Sweet syndrome, which can be an acute reactive overlay on an underlying malignancy or infection. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])
- **"Monocytes love tissue"** — a blood [[Monocytosis|monocytosis]] and a skin infiltrate can be one process, since monocytes are the tissue-dwelling macrophage lineage; the nodule can be the accessible window on a marrow disease. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])
- **A full-body skin exam can put the answer on the surface**, and biopsy of an accessible nodule can be the fastest route to a diagnosis buried under expensive imaging (see [[Return to the Bedside]]). ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])

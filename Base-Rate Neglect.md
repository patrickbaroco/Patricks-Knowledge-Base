## Definition

Base-rate neglect is failing to weight prior probability — chasing a vivid, specific, or [[Availability Heuristic|available]] diagnosis while ignoring how common it actually is in this population. It is the bias that [[Base Rate|base-rate reasoning]] guards against: where base rate says "start from the frequent and spend energy in proportion," neglect jumps to the zebra on the strength of a salient feature. The corrective is not to ignore the striking finding but to keep the prior in view and let specific data *adjust* it rather than override it.

## How it operates

- **Priors reorder the list before any test.** Demographics and frequency can down-weight a diagnosis without a single result — using prior probability, not new data, to re-rank.
- **Base rate as a starting weight, then tempered.** Invoke the common cause first, then let host- and tempo-specific data move it; neglect is when the specific feature erases the prior entirely.
- **It is the mirror image of [[Availability Heuristic|availability]] and [[Representativeness Heuristic|representativeness]].** A recent case or a good "fit" pulls toward a diagnosis its base rate does not support.
- **Run toward the can't-miss, then look for the landmines.** Prioritizing the dangerous-and-plausible while still respecting frequency keeps rare-but-lethal and common-and-likely both in view.

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **Demographics steer without a test.** Rabih down-weights primary hyperparathyroidism purely on demographics (an older man versus the classic middle-aged woman) and elevates the "two I's" as the causes that fit an asymptomatic, indolent presentation — prior probability reordering the list. ([[RLR 007 - Hypercalcemia|RLR 007]])
- **Base rate used, then tempered.** The base rate (lung cancer as the most common US malignancy) prioritizes adenocarcinoma, immediately tempered by the patient's non-smoking status and the competing paraprotein data — base rate as a starting weight, not a verdict. ([[RLR 011 - Fever and Chronic Consolidation|RLR 011]])
- **Run-toward-the-can't-miss, then landmines.** The case models prioritizing the dangerous, plausible diagnosis while continuing to scan for the less obvious contributors — the discipline flagged for the bias catalog alongside anchoring and framing. ([[RLR 008 - Dyspnea|RLR 008]])

## Counter-moves

- **State the prior out loud** before reacting to a striking feature, and rank the differential by frequency first ([[RLR 007 - Hypercalcemia|RLR 007]]).
- **Let specifics adjust, not erase, the base rate** — temper the common answer with host and tempo rather than abandoning it ([[RLR 011 - Fever and Chronic Consolidation|RLR 011]]).

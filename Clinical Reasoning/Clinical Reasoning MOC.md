`[[Aaron Berkowitz]]` was linked as a note target in CPS 199, 263, 339, 351 — all four in prose, none in footers. Unlinked to plain text; people belong in frontmatter, not the concept graph.

**Deliberately NOT merged:** `[[Occam's Razor]]` (5 episodes). It co-occurs with both [[Unifying Diagnosis]] and [[Hickam's Dictum]] in three footers, which reads as the classic Occam/Hickam opposition rather than redundancy. Kept as its own link target and promoted to the build list — the principle and its clinical application are worth separating.

**Standing vocabulary, keep as-is:** `availability-heuristic` (never *availability-bias*), LIED mnemonic ("Lytes", not "Lights"), Nar = Rabih's dog and stays off frontmatter.

**A second structural bug, swept the same day:** six aliased links were written with a single closing bracket — `[[Anchoring Bias|Anchoring / fixation]` instead of `]]` — in RLR 039, RLR 059, and CPS 312. These render as literal text, so the concept link is silently lost and no link audit sees it. All six fixed. Detect with `\[\[[^\]\|]+\|[^\]]*\](?!\])`.

**Left alone deliberately:** four coined titles carry an internal quoted phrase and are consistent, not malformed — `[["Circle of Ventilation" - A Schema for Hypercapnia]]`, `[["Pumps, Pipes, Plasma" - A Framework for Stroke Workup Despite Therapeutic Anticoagulation]]`, `[["Day One Hospital Problem" Framework]]`, `[["Evolution Over CT" - Imaging Restraint in a Host Capable of Localizing Symptoms]]`. They resolve fine, but quotes in filenames are awkward; worth retitling if you ever build them.

**Watch for this bug class:** wikilinks written with quotation marks inside the brackets can never resolve, and the single-bracket `["…"]` variant is not a link at all — it renders as literal text and is invisible to a link audit. Three separate concepts were lost this way. A vault-wide sweep on 2026-08-20 found and fixed every instance; re-run `grep -rn '\[\[\s*"' --include=*.md .` after any batch of new notes.

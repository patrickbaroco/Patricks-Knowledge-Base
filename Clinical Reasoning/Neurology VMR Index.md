**Conventions & notes**
- The site's "Episode NNN" number **is** the vault's `CPS NNN` number (e.g. Episode 402 → note `CPS 402`). Titles here are the site's; retitle to the presenting-problem + diagnosis form when a note is built.
- Each episode page has a **public mp3** (WordPress uploads). No written transcript is hosted — transcript must be produced from the audio, then pasted with `CPS NNN:` to trigger a note.
- ⭐ = altered mental status / encephalopathy (Patrick's favored [[Localization × Time Course|localization × time course]] teaching turf — see [[Altered Mental Status]]). ◆ = movement/myoclonus or cerebellar/ataxia/vestibular/gait (ties to [[Myoclonus]], [[Ataxia]], [[MIST Mnemonic]], [[RLR 037 - Episodic Lightheadedness and Functional Neurological Disorder|RLR 037]]).
- This tag covers **Ep 193 (Aug 2021) → Ep 447 (Feb 2026), 35 episodes**. Earlier neuro VMRs (2020–early 2021, incl. your **CPS 153**) predate the tag and aren't listed here.

## Transcription workflow (MacWhisper)

- MacWhisper Pro's confirmed URL input is **YouTube**; its URL fetcher is yt-dlp-based, so **pasting a direct .mp3 link very likely works too** — test once, and if it transcribes, that's the universal "point to the link" method (no downloading).
- The ⭐ AMS episodes are **audio-only (no YouTube)**, so use the mp3 link (or one-click Safari download → drag into MacWhisper / a **Watch Folder** that exports to this vault).
- Batch: MacWhisper Pro queues multiple files; Watch Folder + its Obsidian export can drop transcripts straight here.
- Then paste the transcript with `CPS NNN:` to trigger the note.

## Pearls

- **Work a non-resolving infection as an [[Antibiotic Failure|antibiotic-failure]] problem: source control, wrong/incomplete diagnosis, or wrong treatment.** The commonest cause of failure in all comers is **source control**, but always re-ask whether the **diagnosis** is wrong *or merely incomplete* (a right diagnosis plus a second process) and whether the **treatment** missed the bug or the dose — and remember [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]] causes *monoarticular* arthritis only *indirectly*, through immunosuppression-related infection.
- **[[Questioning the Diagnosis|Question carried diagnoses]] — some deserve a standing red flag.** A "lupus" in a patient never followed and never on hydroxychloroquine, and three prior "septic arthritides," are claims to verify, not accept; **[[ANA-Negative Lupus|ANA-negative lupus with normal complement essentially does not exist]]**, just as sarcoidosis (a diagnosis of exclusion) and label-only "COPD/asthma" without PFTs warrant re-checking.
- **Recurrent [[Septic Arthritis|septic arthritis]] in a young host should make you question the *host*.** Three joint infections is odd with a competent immune system; pursue [[HIV|HIV]] and, when respiratory/sinus/skin infections cluster, a **[[Primary Immunodeficiency|primary immunodeficiency]]**. And diagnose septic arthritis honestly: [[Synovial Fluid Analysis|synovial WBC >50k neutrophil-predominant]] means *crystal or infection*, the gram stain is insensitive, and a negative culture leaves it a question mark — not an exclusion.
- **Multiple simultaneous nutritional deficiencies mean [[Malabsorption|diffuse malabsorption]], not a pinpoint cause.** Coexisting [[Iron Deficiency|iron deficiency]] (transferrin sat 9%) and B12 <100 point to a broad gut process rather than a single mechanism (e.g., pernicious anemia); pair that with recurrent monoarthritis and an [[Acneform Rash|acneform rash]] and the search narrows to the [[Spondyloarthropathy|spondyloarthropathy]] / autoinflammatory family.
- **Bridge from the familiar [[Neutrophilic Dermatoses|neutrophilic dermatoses]] to their neutrophilic-*arthritis* cousins.** [[Sweet Syndrome|Sweet syndrome]] and [[Pyoderma Gangrenosum|pyoderma gangrenosum]] are the taught examples of innate-immune/neutrophil damage confined to *superficial* structures (skin, joints); the same biology drives **[[SAPHO Syndrome|SAPHO]], [[CRMO|CRMO]], [[PAPA Syndrome|PAPA]], [[PASH Syndrome|PASH]], and [[Behçet Disease|Behçet]]**, whose cutaneous marks are pustules and acne — and **bilateral [[Sacroiliitis|sacroiliitis]]** (unlike unilateral, which suggests infection) confidently marks a spondyloarthropathy.
- **[[Serologies Reflect the Immune System|A serologic test is only as good as the immune system that makes it]] — and one antibody deficiency can [[Unifying Diagnosis|unify]] everything.** With profound [[Hypogammaglobulinemia|hypogammaglobulinemia]] (no B cells), the negative ANA, negative celiac serologies, and undetectable vaccine [[Antibody Titers|titers]] are *uninterpretable*, not reassuring. An **[[Antibody Deficiency|antibody deficiency]]** (post-[[Rituximab|rituximab]] or [[Common Variable Immunodeficiency|CVID]]) explains the recurrent culture-negative septic arthritis, pneumonias, sinusitis, and malabsorption at once — so **everything the patient reported, he truly had** — and the [[Law of Proportionality|law of proportionality]] still argues about whether a single rituximab dose 12 years earlier could be the whole story.

## Problem Representation

A 24-year-old man with biopsy-confirmed pediatric-onset lupus nephritis (treated once, in childhood, with prednisone, hydroxychloroquine, and rituximab, then lost to follow-up) presents with a third episode of destructive, recurrent monoarticular arthritis of the left knee, persisting despite washout and antibiotics, alongside 13 lbs of weight loss, night sweats, an acneform rash, coexisting iron and B12 deficiency (diffuse malabsorption), bone-origin alkaline-phosphatase elevation, extensive multifocal osteomyelitis with cartilage destruction, culture-negative synovial neutrophilia, and bilateral sacroiliac sclerosis. His current lupus serologies (ANA, dsDNA, complement, urinalysis) are negative/normal, and a broad autoimmune and infectious workup is unrevealing. A history of recurrent pneumonias and chronic sinusitis reframes the syndrome as a humoral immunodeficiency: immunoglobulins are profoundly low (IgA 10, IgG 75, IgM 20) with undetectable protective vaccine titers, rendering his other serologies uninterpretable. With a negative 400-gene panel, no pre-lupus childhood infections, and a normal IgG before lupus treatment, the leading diagnosis is **persistent hypogammaglobulinemia after rituximab** (common variable immunodeficiency not excluded), which unifies the recurrent culture-negative septic arthritis, pneumonias, sinusitis, and malabsorption — treated with IVIG, with clinical recovery.

## Case Narrative

Guest **Dr. Alec Rezigh** — long part of the CP Solvers community — presents a case he picked up on service. A **24-year-old man** with pediatric-onset **[[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|systemic lupus erythematosus]]** and depression presents with **left knee swelling and pain**, one week after discharge from a neighboring hospital where two weeks of progressive knee swelling led to a **washout and synovectomy** and oral antibiotics for presumed **[[Septic Arthritis|septic arthritis]]**. He denies fevers and systemic symptoms but reports **13 lbs of weight loss over two months** and **intermittent night sweats**.

Rabih frames the presentation as an **[[Antibiotic Failure|antibiotic-failure]]** problem: the swelling likely reflects *unmitigated disease* rather than post-procedural inflammation, and the differential for antibiotic failure in all comers is **source control** (the commonest), a **diagnosis problem** (completely wrong, *or* right-but-incomplete — an antibiotic complication, a post-obstructive process behind a cancer), or a **treatment problem** (wrong bug, wrong dose). He anchors an important teaching point up front: **[[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]] causes polyarthritis, not monoarthritis**, and its only link to a monoarticular joint is *indirect* — immunosuppression predisposing to **[[Septic Arthritis|infectious arthritis]]**.

The history complicates every assumption. The lupus was diagnosed at **12** in Southern California after a **blistering rash** and blood tests; he was treated, then **lost to follow-up** after moving to Texas and never treated since. **Three years ago** he had "septic arthritis" of the right shoulder; **one year ago**, the right knee — both "recovered" with antibiotics and surgery, no organism ever recalled. His mother and sister both had lupus and both **died of infectious complications**. He takes Bactrim, cefdinir, and tramadol for the knee. Reza [[Questioning the Diagnosis|questions the carried diagnoses]]: a real lupus patient would be followed by rheumatology and on hydroxychloroquine, so an untreated "lupus" is either unconfirmed or reflects care barriers — he'd send ANA, complement, dsDNA, anti-Smith and hunt for outside records. And **three episodes of septic arthritis** in a young man is odd for a competent immune system: either the earlier diagnoses were wrong, or the **host is immunocompromised** (he'd check HIV, the CBC/differential, vaccination history, and for recurrent respiratory or skin infections). He teaches how [[Synovial Fluid Analysis|septic arthritis is actually diagnosed]] — synovial-fluid appearance, a **WBC north of 50,000 neutrophil-predominant** narrowing to *crystal or infection*, an **insensitive gram stain**, and cultures — and that a negative gram stain/culture leaves it a *question mark*, not an exclusion. The **13-lb weight loss**, he notes, doesn't fit an acute septic joint and hints at a systemic illness. Rabih distills it: *all we truly know is recurrent monoarticular arthritis* — everything else must be questioned.

The **exam** adds an acneform clue. He is afebrile (HR 108), thin (BMI 23), with **scattered mildly erythematous papules over the forehead, cheeks, and upper back**, well-healed scars from prior washouts, and a swollen, warm, exquisitely tender **left knee** with a large effusion and pitting edema to the foot (from the [[Synovitis|synovitis]]); other joints are normal. Rabih plays the **[[Base Rate|base rate]]** carefully: an [[Acneform Rash|acneform rash]] at 24 is overwhelmingly **true-true-and-unrelated primary acne** — *except* that the base rate no longer applies once there is **recurrent, unexplained monoarticular arthritis**, at which point the acne becomes a potential clue to the **[[Spondyloarthropathy|spondyloarthropathies]]** (ankylosing spondylitis, psoriatic, IBD-related, reactive — though he lacks axial back pain) and especially the **secondary** ones that prominently feature acne: **[[Behçet Disease|Behçet]]**, **[[PAPA Syndrome|PAPA]]** (pyogenic arthritis, pyoderma gangrenosum, acne), and **[[PASH Syndrome|PASH]]** (pyoderma gangrenosum, acne, suppurative hidradenitis).

**Labs** deepen it. A **microcytic anemia** (Hgb 9.2, MCV 75) with **iron saturation 9%** but **ferritin 213** tells Reza there is **[[Iron Deficiency|iron deficiency]] *and* inflammation** coexisting; a **B12 <100** makes it **multiple nutritional deficiencies** — and multiple deficiencies mean **[[Malabsorption|diffuse malabsorption]]**, not a *pinpoint* cause like pernicious anemia. The **normal WBC** and afebrile state remove two-thirds of the classic septic-arthritis triad (fever, impaired ROM, leukocytosis). The **[[Alkaline Phosphatase|alkaline phosphatase]] of 207** with normal transaminases and known bony disease is **bone-origin** (later confirmed by a normal GGT). Imaging seals the tissue map: the knee **arthrocentesis** shows WBC 22,700 (90% neutrophils), **no crystals, negative gram stain, and negative bacterial/fungal/AFB cultures**, and the outside **MRI** reveals a severely thickened synovium, **extensive multifocal [[Osteomyelitis|osteomyelitis]]** through the distal femur/patella/proximal tibia, and **complete cartilage loss with cortical erosion**; surgical pathology shows **chronic inflammation with negative cultures**.

Rabih lands on the **[[Spondyloarthropathy|secondary spondyloarthropathies]]**: recurrent multifocal osteomyelitis + neutrophilic synovial pleocytosis + acneform rash is the signature of **[[SAPHO Syndrome|SAPHO]], [[CRMO|CRMO]], [[PAPA Syndrome|PAPA]], [[PASH Syndrome|PASH]], and [[Behçet Disease|Behçet]]**. When the workup returns **negative ANA and dsDNA with normal complement and no proteinuria**, Reza [[Questioning the Diagnosis|confidently excludes lupus]] — **[[ANA-Negative Lupus|ANA-negative lupus with normal complement essentially doesn't exist]]**, and the absent albuminuria means the kidneys aren't threatened — while a normal celiac panel and negative pernicious-anemia antibodies keep the malabsorption **diffuse**. Rabih then supplies the case's most beautiful [[Analogical Reasoning|analogy]]: extrapolate from the familiar **[[Neutrophilic Dermatoses|neutrophilic dermatoses]]** ([[Sweet Syndrome|Sweet]], [[Pyoderma Gangrenosum|pyoderma gangrenosum]]) — innate-immune, neutrophil-driven damage confined to **superficial structures (skin and joints)** — to their neutrophilic-**arthritis** cousins, whose skin manifestations are pustules and acne. **Bilateral [[Sacroiliitis|sacroiliac sclerosis]]** (bilateral → spondyloarthropathy; unilateral would suggest infection) makes him confident this is a **systemic disease of skin, bone, and joint**, most likely a **secondary spondyloarthropathy** potentially treatable with **[[Anakinra|IL-1 therapy]]**.

Then the case pivots. Repeat history reveals that after the lupus diagnosis he was **hospitalized three times for pneumonia**, and his father reports **recurrent sinus infections** over the same period. Reza names the meta-lesson — **framing the problem matters more than knowing everything** (his "yellow nail syndrome / dystrophic nails" anecdote) — and the recurrent **encapsulated-organism** infections (pneumonia, sinusitis, septic arthritis, opsonized by immunoglobulin) force a **[[Antibody Deficiency|re-pivot toward immunodeficiency]]**: he'd send immunoglobulins, lymphocyte subsets, and **vaccine-response testing**, and asks whether an [[Antibody Deficiency|antibody-deficient state]] could **[[Unifying Diagnosis|unify]]** the infections *and* the malabsorption (as **[[Common Variable Immunodeficiency|CVID]]** can). Rabih agrees the recurrent-infection history is "so heavy" it demands a different direction, recalling that many **GI disorders cause arthritis-dermatitis syndromes** (celiac — from Kirtan's recent case — tropical sprue, IBD).

The immunology confirms it. **Immunoglobulins are profoundly low** (IgA 10, IgG 75, IgM 20, on repeat), with **undetectable diphtheria, tetanus, and pneumococcal [[Antibody Titers|antibody titers]]** and a mildly low neutrophil oxidative burst; lymphocyte subsets are otherwise largely intact. Reza teaches the elegant point that you don't always need to *vaccinate and recheck* — you can simply **measure the protective antibodies everyone should already carry** (tetanus, diphtheria), and their absence means either no vaccination or **no antibody response**. Immunoglobulins come from **B lymphocytes**, from marrow lymphoid precursors — so this is a **humoral (antibody) [[Antibody Deficiency|deficiency]]**, [[Common Variable Immunodeficiency|CVID]] vs an alternative, with secondary causes ([[Rituximab|rituximab]], myeloma, nephrotic/protein-losing states) to exclude. Then Reza states the case's crowning pearl: **[[Serologies Reflect the Immune System|serologic tests are only as good as the immune system]]** — with no B cells, the **negative ANA and negative celiac serologies were never interpretable**, a re-reading that dissolves the earlier "lupus excluded" and "celiac excluded" conclusions.

Childhood **records** then shatter and rebuild the story: at 12 he truly had a **blistering rash, hematuria and proteinuria, positive ANA and dsDNA**, and a **renal biopsy confirming class IV [[Lupus Nephritis|lupus nephritis]]**, treated with **prednisone, hydroxychloroquine, and [[Rituximab|rituximab]]** — with a **normal IgG *before* treatment**. So the lupus was **real** (it met the gold standard), and the immunodeficiency is **acquired**. Rabih wonders whether the rituximab "worked too well," permanently shutting down both the lupus *and* the immunoglobulins; Reza, learning the patient received rituximab **only once, at 12**, invokes the **[[Law of Proportionality|law of proportionality]]** — going "on record" that one dose 12 years ago shouldn't cause this (with a self-deprecating nod to a past overconfident "it's not TB"). Regardless, the management is the same: **[[IVIG|IVIG]]** (pre-medicated given absent IgA and anaphylaxis risk) and eventual immunosuppression — and, crucially, **the negative serologies reflect absent B cells, not absent disease**.

The **final synthesis**: with a **negative 400-gene panel**, **no infections before the lupus diagnosis**, and a **normal pre-treatment IgG**, the rheumatology/immunology team's leading diagnosis is **persistent hypogammaglobulinemia after rituximab** — though **CVID cannot be fully excluded** (autoimmunity can precede CVID, more often in adults). The hypogammaglobulinemia unifies the recurrent, **culture-negative** septic arthritis (immunodeficient joints often sterilize after a single antibiotic dose, and universal PCR was never sent), the pneumonias, the sinusitis, and the malabsorption. On **IVIG** he **gained weight and is doing well**. Reza's closing reflection: **everything the patient said he had, he actually had** — lupus, and recurrent infections — and the enduring lesson is that a serologic test is only as informative as the immune machinery that produces it, with both hosts humbled by the sheer cognitive work and dedication the case demanded.

### Illness Scripts and Bias

- **[[Antibiotic Failure|Antibiotic-failure]] framework as the entry schema.** Source control vs a diagnosis that's *wrong or incomplete* vs a treatment problem organizes a non-resolving "septic arthritis," and the up-front teaching that [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|lupus]] reaches a *monoarticular* joint only *indirectly* (immunosuppression → infection) reframes the whole case.
- **[[Questioning the Diagnosis|Questioning carried diagnoses]].** The "lupus" (untreated, unfollowed) and the three "septic arthritides" are treated as claims to verify; **[[ANA-Negative Lupus|ANA-negative lupus with normal complement]]** is a near-impossibility, echoing the standing red flags on sarcoidosis and label-only COPD/asthma.
- **[[Base Rate|Base rate]] and its suspension.** The [[Acneform Rash|acneform rash]] is true-true-and-unrelated *until* an unexplained recurrent arthritis suspends the base rate and promotes it to a clue — a disciplined use of "when the syndrome is weird, all bets are off."
- **[[Analogical Reasoning|Analogical reasoning]] from [[Neutrophilic Dermatoses|neutrophilic dermatoses]].** Extrapolating from the taught [[Sweet Syndrome|Sweet]]/[[Pyoderma Gangrenosum|pyoderma gangrenosum]] biology (innate-immune, neutrophil damage confined to skin and joints) to the neutrophilic **arthritides** ([[SAPHO Syndrome|SAPHO]], [[PAPA Syndrome|PAPA]], [[PASH Syndrome|PASH]], [[Behçet Disease|Behçet]]) — with **bilateral [[Sacroiliitis|sacroiliitis]]** as the confident spondyloarthropathy marker.
- **[[Test Interpretation|Test interpretation]] throughout.** [[Synovial Fluid Analysis|Synovial fluid]] parameters (>50k = crystal/infection; insensitive gram stain; culture-negative ≠ excluded); iron studies (saturation vs ferritin-as-acute-phase); [[Alkaline Phosphatase|ALP]] bone-vs-liver by GGT; **[[Malabsorption|pinpoint vs diffuse]]** malabsorption from the *number* of deficiencies; and checking **baseline protective [[Antibody Titers|vaccine titers]]** rather than vaccinate-and-recheck.
- **[[Serologies Reflect the Immune System|Serologic tests are only as good as the immune system]] — the crowning pearl.** Profound [[Hypogammaglobulinemia|hypogammaglobulinemia]] (no B cells) makes a negative ANA and negative celiac serologies *uninterpretable*, retroactively dissolving two "exclusions" and rescuing the true lupus history.
- **[[Unifying Diagnosis|Unifying diagnosis]] (anti-Hickam resolution).** A single [[Antibody Deficiency|antibody deficiency]] explains the recurrent culture-negative septic arthritis, pneumonias, sinusitis, and malabsorption — so *everything the patient reported was true* — the payoff of parsimony after a long plural differential.
- **[[Law of Proportionality|Law of proportionality]] and [[Diagnostic Humility|humility]].** The rituximab debate (one dose 12 years prior — Reza's doubt vs the team's leading "persistent post-rituximab" conclusion), records that shatter working hypotheses, and "framing beats knowing everything" close the case in earned humility.

## Teaching Discussion

- Approach a non-resolving infection as antibiotic failure: weigh source control first, but re-ask whether the diagnosis is wrong or only incomplete and whether the treatment missed the organism or the dose — and remember lupus produces monoarthritis only indirectly through immunosuppression-related infection.
- Verify carried diagnoses rather than inheriting them: an untreated, unfollowed "lupus" and repeated "septic arthritis" are claims to test; ANA-negative lupus with normal complement essentially does not exist, and diagnoses like sarcoidosis and unconfirmed COPD/asthma deserve the same skepticism.
- Diagnose septic arthritis on its merits: a synovial WBC over 50,000 neutrophil-predominant narrows to crystal or infection, the gram stain is insensitive, and negative cultures do not exclude it — while recurrent joint infections in a young person should prompt a hunt for immunodeficiency.
- Read multiple simultaneous nutritional deficiencies as diffuse malabsorption, not a pinpoint cause, and interpret iron studies by transferrin saturation versus ferritin (an acute-phase reactant) to separate iron deficiency from inflammation.
- Suspend the base rate deliberately: an acneform rash is usually incidental, but with unexplained recurrent monoarthritis it becomes a clue to the spondyloarthropathies and their acne-featuring secondary forms (SAPHO, CRMO, PAPA, PASH, Behçet); bilateral sacroiliitis confidently marks a spondyloarthropathy, whereas unilateral suggests infection.
- Use the neutrophilic dermatoses (Sweet, pyoderma gangrenosum) as an anchor for the neutrophilic arthritides — innate-immune, neutrophil-mediated disease confined to skin and joints — when arthritis, osteitis, and pustular/acneform skin coexist.
- Pivot on a history of recurrent encapsulated-organism infections (pneumonia, sinusitis, septic arthritis) toward an antibody deficiency; confirm with immunoglobulin levels and protective vaccine titers, and remember that immunoglobulins derive from B cells.
- Hold the crowning principle: a serologic test is only as good as the immune system that makes it — profound hypogammaglobulinemia renders ANA and other serologies uninterpretable — and let a single antibody deficiency (post-rituximab or CVID) unify recurrent infections and malabsorption, treating with IVIG while distinguishing acquired from primary causes.

# Podcast Notes, Nov 2009 (2)

PACE trial  
implanted bivent pacers in 177 patients with normal EF. followed for 12 months.  EF lower in RV pacing: 7.4% less (absolute). LVEDP higher in RV paced group: 8.1mm hg. at the end, 8 patients in RV group had EF \<45 vs 1 in bivent group.  
  
afib was an exclusion criteria, and patients with CAD were excluded as well.  also, 2 pts in bivent grp had 2 go to RV grp 2' diaphragmatic stim.  
  
no diff in hospitalizations, mortality, or cv events but not powered to detect significant diff  
  
reason for adverse events likely 2' rv apical pacing. this causes a LBBB, leading to electromechanical delay in contraction (systolic dyssynchrony) and subsequently to assymmetric hypertrophy and increased MR and decr EF.

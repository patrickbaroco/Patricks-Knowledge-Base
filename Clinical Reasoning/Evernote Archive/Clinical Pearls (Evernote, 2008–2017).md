## Liddle's syndrome

Liddle's syndrome: a syndrome of apparent mineralocorticoid excess where a mutation in the distal tubule sodium channel prevents it from being shut off, so excess sodium is re-absorbed. This leads to salt-sensitive hypertension, AME but with low plasma aldosterone concentrations and low renin activity levels. Sine qua non is sensitivity to amiloride and triamterene, which block the affected sodium channels.

*Saved 2009-07-17*

## Rheumatoid arthritis pearls

Rheumatoid arthritis:  
\- associatied with a vasculitis, and can see mononeuritis multiplex (foot drop, wrist drop, etc)  
  
\- cervical instability. Get flexion/extension films before intubation. With neuro deficits, don't forget to consider the neck.  
  
\- RF also (+) with viral hepatitis and subacute bacterial endocarditis. If RF (+) but no RA, check hepatitis panel

*Saved 2010-02-17*

## Constipation in Parkinsons

From: Parkinsons Disease and Non-Motor Dysfunction, Pfeiffer and Bodis-Wollner

  

\-Start with Miralax

\-Irritant laxatives like senna

\-Neostigmine for Ogilvie's; consider pyridostigmine orally for 6-10 weeks

\-Misoprostol, especially for left-colon disease

\-Colchicine

*Saved 2015-02-09*

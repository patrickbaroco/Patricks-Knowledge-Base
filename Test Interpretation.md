## Definition

Test interpretation is reading a result against the mechanism and context of the disease rather than off the report alone. The same "clean" or "high" value means different things in different beds: a negative study's power depends on the anatomy of the disease it is meant to exclude, a hormone level is uninterpretable without its partner metabolite, and paired urine or serum studies must be read as an ordered sequence rather than a bundle. The discipline is to ask *what would make this value mean what I think it means* before acting on it.

Aaron Berkowitz refers to it as "biopsy your guy". [[CPS 153 - Intermittent Altered Mental Status and Non-Lesional Autoimmune Epilepsy|CPS 153]]

## How it operates

- **A negative test's value is conditioned on the disease's mechanism.** Clean coronaries argue down ACS (coronary disease is in-situ and local); clean cerebral vessels do *not* argue down stroke (the brain is as embolic as it is thrombotic).
- **Some values are uninterpretable alone.** A high 1,25-vitamin D means ingestion or pathological conversion — you can't tell which without the 25-hydroxy level.
- **Read paired studies as a lock, in order.** Urine osm establishes ADH is on; urine sodium establishes aldosterone is off; only their conjunction yields inappropriate ADH.
- **Guard the preconditions.** A urine sodium is meaningless on diuretics or in ATN — check the interpretive context before trusting the number.

## Examples from RLR episodes

- **Negative test by mechanism.** A clean CTA excludes large-vessel thrombus and profiles risk but does not exclude cardioembolism — clean brain vessels still permit an embolus, unlike clean coronaries and ACS. ([[RLR 027 - Dizziness and Peripheral Vertigo|RLR 027]])
- **Pair the metabolites.** A high 1,25-vitamin D is uninterpretable without the 25-hydroxy value — high 25 points to ingestion, low 25 to granulomatous/PTHrP conversion. ([[RLR 028 - Hypercalcemia and Vitamin D Toxicity|RLR 028]])
- **The two-step lock.** Urine osm then urine sodium, read in sequence and only off diuretics, is what establishes inappropriate ADH. ([[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])

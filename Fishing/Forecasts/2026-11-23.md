## Week summary

- Still very dry. The weekend rain never arrived, and water everywhere is low and clear.
- Cold early in the week: Tuesday night in the Valley could drop into the teens. Rain is possible Wednesday night and Thursday, and the post says it could be significant.
- Best trout bets are managed and Delayed Harvest water (Passage Creek, Back Creek, North River, the Pedlar, South River) and the spring creeks (Buffalo, Beaver, Mossy). Fish the warmer hours.

## Water

- Overall: very dry, everything low and clear; the weekend rain did not come. Possible significant rain Wednesday night into Thursday.
- Delayed Harvest and managed trout water: Passage Creek, Back Creek, North River, the Pedlar and South River are among the best bets.
- Spring creeks: Buffalo, Beaver and Mossy fish well in the cold because spring water holds warmer.
- Brook trout streams: fish are spawning in low water; leave them alone for now.
- James and Shenandoah: musky water, low and clear, so fish can be shy.
- Mountain lakes: Elkhorn and Switzer, best on a calm day.

- Trout surface activity: fish may cruise for small mayflies or midges, especially on overcast days. The post names no specific hatches or patterns.

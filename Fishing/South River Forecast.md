# South River forecast

Rewritten each morning. The Now, Today, Tomorrow and Weekend headings are read by the Today dashboard.

## Now

The Waynesboro gauge (WAYV2) is reporting again and read 22 cfs and 2.32 ft at 4:15am, the same as Monday's last reading and slightly below the 25 cfs logged a week ago. With no rain for a week and the NWS holding Waynesboro flat at about 2.3 ft and 20 cfs, the river is at a steady, very low base flow and should be very clear. After a clear night near 30° the water is cold this morning and should warm to a comfortable range for trout by mid-afternoon.

## Today

Friday is sunny with a high of 68°, a light northeast breeze and no rain. Fish from late morning to about 3pm, working the deeper pools and seams at Ridgeview Park and in the catch-and-release water from Wayne Avenue to North Park with small nymphs on a long, light leader, and stay low and back from the bank, since full sun on clear, shallow water makes the fish wary. Mossy Creek puts the best window at 2 to 3pm, when water temperature peaks just after the air.

## Tomorrow

Saturday is partly sunny and comfortable with a high of 64°, a light east-northeast breeze and no rain. The river should stay low and clear, so the same afternoon plan holds, and the thin clouds may make the fish in the clear pools a little less skittish than today.

## Weekend

Saturday, November 21, is partly sunny with a high of 64° and no rain, and Sunday, November 22, turns cloudy and cooler with a high of 58° and a 30% chance of drizzle that is forecast to bring no measurable total. The river should stay at base flow both days, so the afternoons will fish best, and Sunday's full cloud cover should help in the clear pools, though the cooler air will keep the water colder.

## Next rain

The next rain is forecast from Wednesday afternoon, November 25 (about 0.15 in, 72% chance, with a high of only 41°), into Thursday morning, November 26 (about 0.17 in, 75%), roughly a third of an inch in all, less than yesterday's forecast showed. After a long dry spell much of it will likely soak in, so any rise is uncertain and probably small, and the NWS forecast does not show one; the cold rain will also chill the water. Friday, November 27, mostly sunny with a high of 55°, and Sunday, November 29, mostly sunny with a high of 55°, look like the best days after it, with Saturday, November 28, mostly cloudy and cooler.

## Regional (Mossy Creek)

- From the week of Nov 16 note (blog post and video); the next post is due Monday, November 23. It called for a dry week with cold nights and mild afternoons, which has held.
- Stocking: no South River stocking listed in the last two weeks. DWR's daily list shows entries from November 11 to 18 with nothing for Augusta County or Waynesboro. Mossy Creek reports thin stocking this year after cutbacks and a major loss of mostly rainbow trout at Coursey Springs Hatchery, so managed water holds fewer fish and more browns.
- Valley water: rivers are low and very clear; stocked and delayed harvest water are among the best bets, warm-water fishing has grown steadily harder, and musky are packed into deep winter pools with forage.
- For stocked trout: fish the warmest two or three hours of the afternoon, take water temperatures in a couple of spots and depths, and concentrate where the most fish were put in. Neither source names fly patterns this week.
- Spring creeks: around 55° groundwater makes them the warmest water as it cools; Mossy Creek's big browns had not been reported feeding after the spawn but were expected any day.

## Water and rules

- Wayne Avenue to 2nd Street (North Park), about 2 miles: catch-and-release stocked trout water, year-round, no bait, single-hook artificial lures only.
- Ridgeview Park: put-and-take stocked water, 6 trout per day, 7-inch minimum.
- Basic Park: youth only, April 1 to June 15.
- Lyndhurst, upstream: about 3 miles of fly-fishing-only water on private land, stocked with fingerlings; free permit required.
- Grand Caverns to Grottoes: stocked trout water.
- Mercury advisory: eat no fish from the South River except trout.

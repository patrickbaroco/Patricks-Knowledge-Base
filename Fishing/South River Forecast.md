# South River forecast

Rewritten each morning. The Now, Today, Tomorrow and Weekend headings are read by the Today dashboard.

## Now

The Waynesboro gauge (WAYV2) read 22 cfs and 2.32 ft at 4:15am, unchanged for six days and a little below the 25 cfs logged a week ago, so the river is holding at a steady, very low base flow and should be very clear. The NWS forecast keeps it flat at about 2.3 ft and 20 cfs until Sunday afternoon, then shows a small bump to 2.4 ft and about 31 cfs with Sunday's rain. After a clear night near 30° the water is cold this morning and should warm to a cool, workable range for trout by early afternoon.

## Today

Saturday is sunny through midday and partly sunny later, with a high of 62° around 2pm, a light east breeze and no rain; clouds thicken after 3pm. Fish from late morning to about 3pm in the deeper pools and seams at Ridgeview Park and in the catch-and-release water from Wayne Avenue to North Park, using small nymphs on a long, light leader and keeping low and back from the bank in the clear, shallow water. Mossy Creek's advice holds: the warmest two or three hours of the afternoon fish best, so carry a thermometer and favor spots near springs and seeps.

## Tomorrow

Sunday is cloudy and cooler with a high of 58° and a 62% chance of light rain, about 0.22 in over roughly three hours. The NWS shows only a small rise from midday, so the river should stay low with at most a slight tint, and the full cloud cover and soft rain should make fish in the clear pools less wary; a small nymph or a lightly weighted streamer through the deeper runs in the afternoon is a reasonable plan.

## Weekend

Next Saturday, November 28, is cloudy with a high of 52° and afternoon showers of about 0.30 in, turning to a rain and snow mix in the evening, and Sunday, November 29, brings occasional rain of about 0.23 in with a high of 57°. Coming after Wednesday's rain, the river could be somewhat up and tinted by then, though the size of any rise is uncertain; Saturday morning before the showers looks like the better window.

## Next rain

Sunday, November 22, brings about 0.24 in (0.22 in by day, 62% chance), and the NWS forecast shows WAYV2 rising only to about 2.4 ft and 31 cfs from Sunday afternoon. A larger rain follows from Wednesday afternoon, November 25, into Wednesday night, about 0.40 in in all with a 94% chance overnight and a high of only 43°; after the long dry spell much of it will likely soak in, so any rise is uncertain and probably modest, and the cold rain will chill the water. Monday, November 23 (partly sunny, 67°), is the best day after Sunday's rain, and Friday, November 27 (sunny, 53°), looks like the best day after Wednesday's, before more rain next weekend.

## Regional (Mossy Creek)

- From the week of Nov 16 note (blog post and video); the next post is due Monday, November 23. It called for a dry week with cold nights and mild afternoons, which has held.
- Stocking: no South River stocking listed in the last two weeks. DWR's daily list covers November 12 to 19 with nothing for Augusta County or Waynesboro, and it notes several streams elsewhere left unstocked or cancelled for low water. Mossy Creek reports thin stocking this year after cutbacks and a major loss of mostly rainbow trout at Coursey Springs Hatchery, so managed water holds fewer fish and more browns.
- Valley water: rivers are low and very clear; stocked and delayed harvest water are among the best bets, warm-water fishing has grown steadily harder, and musky are packed into deep winter pools with forage.
- For stocked trout: fish the warmest two or three hours of the afternoon, take water temperatures in a couple of spots and depths, and concentrate where the most fish were put in. Neither source names fly patterns this week.
- Spring creeks: around 55° groundwater makes them the warmest water as it cools; Mossy Creek's big browns had not been reported feeding after the spawn but were expected any day.

## Water and rules

- Wayne Avenue to 2nd Street (North Park), about 2 miles: catch-and-release stocked trout water, year-round, no bait, single-hook artificial lures only.
- Ridgeview Park: put-and-take stocked water, 6 trout per day, 7-inch minimum.
- Basic Park: youth only, April 1 to June 15.
- Lyndhurst, upstream: about 3 miles of fly-fishing-only water on private land, stocked with fingerlings; free permit required.
- Grand Caverns to Grottoes: stocked trout water.
- Mercury advisory: eat no fish from the South River except trout.

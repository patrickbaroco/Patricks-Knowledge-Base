# South River forecast

Rewritten each morning. The Now, Today, Tomorrow and Weekend headings are read by the Today dashboard.

## Now

The Waynesboro gauge (WAYV2) read 23 cfs and 2.33 ft at 4:15am, down one from yesterday and one above the 22 cfs logged a week ago, so the river is flat at a very low base flow and should be clear. Yesterday's light showers made no real rise, and the NWS forecast issued Sunday morning holds the gauge level at 2.3 ft and about 20 cfs through Wednesday morning. After a mild weekend the water should be cool and comfortable for trout today, but clear, cold nights on Monday and Tuesday will chill it noticeably by midweek.

## Today

Monday is partly sunny and warm, with a high of 68°, little chance of rain and only a light west-northwest breeze. In this low, clear water under brighter skies, fish the warmest hours from late morning to mid-afternoon, keep low and back from the bank, and work small nymphs or a lightly weighted streamer through the deeper runs and pools at Ridgeview Park and in the catch-and-release water from Wayne Avenue to North Park. Mossy Creek's advice to fish around 2 to 3pm, when the water is warmest, fits a day like this.

## Tomorrow

Tuesday is mostly sunny and much cooler, with a high of 51°, no rain and a north-northeast breeze gusting to about 15 mph, after a clear night near 34°. The river should be unchanged and very clear, and the colder water will slow the fish, so wait for the afternoon, fish slowly and deep in the pools, and expect fewer, gentler takes than today.

## Weekend

Saturday, November 28, has intervals of clouds and sun, a high of 54° and little chance of rain, and Sunday, November 29, brings occasional light rain of about 0.18 in with a high of 57°. Coming two days after the heavy Thanksgiving rain, Saturday should find the river still elevated but falling and beginning to clear, and it is the better of the two days; fish the afternoon and check the gauge first, since the size of the rise is uncertain.

## Next rain

The next meaningful rain starts Wednesday night, November 25, about 0.58 in (92% chance), and continues as steady rain on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, about 0.94 in more (99% chance), with north-northeast gusts near 30 mph and a high of 48°; that is roughly 1.5 in in all, the largest rain in this log. The NWS forecast for WAYV2 ends Wednesday morning, before the rain, and after the long dry spell some of it will soak in, so the size of the rise is uncertain, though this much rain should bring the river up and color it for a day or two, and the cold rain will chill the water. Friday, November 27, is breezy with gusts near 29 mph, so Saturday, November 28, and the sunny Monday, November 30 (58°), look like the best days as the water falls and clears.

## Regional (Mossy Creek)

- From the week of Nov 16 note (blog post and video); the next post is due today, Monday, November 23. It called for a dry week with cold nights and mild afternoons, which held through the weekend apart from Sunday's light showers.
- Stocking: no South River stocking listed in the last two weeks. DWR's daily list covers November 13 to 20 with nothing for Augusta County or Waynesboro; the only South River entry (November 20) is the Rockbridge County water, not this river. Nearby valley stockings in that window were in Shenandoah, Frederick and Warren counties, and DWR has canceled two November 28 stockings in Wythe County for low flow. Mossy Creek reports thin stocking this year after cutbacks and a major loss of mostly rainbow trout at Coursey Springs Hatchery, so managed water holds fewer fish and more browns.
- Valley water: rivers are low and very clear; stocked and delayed harvest water are among the best bets, warm-water fishing has grown steadily harder, and musky are packed into deep winter pools with forage.
- For stocked trout: fish the warmest two or three hours of the afternoon, take water temperatures in a couple of spots and depths, and concentrate where the most fish were put in. Neither source names fly patterns this week.
- Spring creeks: around 55° groundwater makes them the warmest water as it cools, which matters more after this week's cold nights; Mossy Creek's big browns had not been reported feeding after the spawn but were expected any day.

## Water and rules

- Wayne Avenue to 2nd Street (North Park), about 2 miles: catch-and-release stocked trout water, year-round, no bait, single-hook artificial lures only.
- Ridgeview Park: put-and-take stocked water, 6 trout per day, 7-inch minimum.
- Basic Park: youth only, April 1 to June 15.
- Lyndhurst, upstream: about 3 miles of fly-fishing-only water on private land, stocked with fingerlings; free permit required.
- Grand Caverns to Grottoes: stocked trout water.
- Mercury advisory: eat no fish from the South River except trout.

# South River forecast

Rewritten each morning. The Now, Today, Tomorrow and Weekend headings are read by the Today dashboard.

## Now

The Waynesboro gauge (WAYV2) read 21 cfs and 2.31 ft at 4:15am, slightly below yesterday's 23 cfs and a week ago's 22 cfs, so the river is flat at a very low base flow and should be very clear this morning. The NWS forecast issued Tuesday morning holds that level through tonight, then shows a sharp rise with the Thanksgiving rain to about 3.3 ft and 240 cfs on Thursday evening. After Tuesday's cool day and a hard freeze last night, the water should be cold this morning and the trout sluggish until the warmest part of the day.

## Today

Wednesday is cloudy, with a high of 46°, a light north-northeast breeze and no rain until late tonight. Fish from late morning to mid-afternoon, slowly and deep with small nymphs through the pools at Ridgeview Park and in the catch-and-release water from Wayne Avenue to North Park, and keep low in the clear water. The cloud cover helps, and Mossy Creek notes that overcast days are when trout are most likely to cruise for small midges or mayflies.

## Tomorrow

Thanksgiving Day is windy and wet, with about 1.2 in of rain (99% chance), a high of 52° and a Wind Advisory for Augusta County from 1am to 1pm, with northeast gusts that could reach 50 to 55 mph and bring down trees. The river will be rising and turning muddy all day toward the forecast peak near 240 cfs in the evening, so this is a day to stay off the water.

## Weekend

Saturday, November 28, has periods of sun and a high of 58°, and Sunday, November 29, has clouds and sun, a high of 59° and a couple of evening showers of about 0.17 in. The river should be falling from Thursday night's peak but still well above its base flow and probably stained on Saturday, which suits slower, deeper water along the edges and larger, more visible flies; Sunday should be lower and clearer. The NWS forecast ends Friday morning, so check the gauge before going.

## Next rain

Rain begins late tonight, Wednesday, November 25, with about 0.70 in (97% chance), and continues through Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, with about 1.19 in more and a few late showers, close to 1.9 in in all and the largest rain in this log. The NWS forecast now covers it: WAYV2 rises to 3.3 ft and 240 cfs from 7pm Thursday to 1am Friday, roughly ten times the current flow, and is still at 3.0 ft and 148 cfs at 7am Friday when the forecast ends; the cold rain will also chill the water further. Friday, November 27, stays breezy with gusts near 29 mph, so Saturday is the first reasonable day, and the mild, mostly sunny stretch from Monday, November 30, to Wednesday, December 2 (53° to 58°), looks best as the water falls and clears.

- Spring creeks: Buffalo, Beaver and Mossy stay warmer and fish well in the cold, and they usually clear sooner than freestone water after rain.

## Water and rules

- Wayne Avenue to 2nd Street (North Park), about 2 miles: catch-and-release stocked trout water, year-round, no bait, single-hook artificial lures only.
- Ridgeview Park: put-and-take stocked water, 6 trout per day, 7-inch minimum.
- Basic Park: youth only, April 1 to June 15.
- Lyndhurst, upstream: about 3 miles of fly-fishing-only water on private land, stocked with fingerlings; free permit required.
- Grand Caverns to Grottoes: stocked trout water.
- Mercury advisory: eat no fish from the South River except trout.

# South River forecast

Rewritten each morning. The Now, Today, Tomorrow and Weekend headings are read by the Today dashboard.

## Now

The Waynesboro gauge (WAYV2) read 23 cfs and 2.33 ft at 4:15am, the same as yesterday and one above the 22 cfs logged a week ago, so the river is flat at a very low base flow and should be very clear. The NWS forecast issued Monday morning holds the gauge at 2.3 ft and about 20 cfs until a first small rise early Thanksgiving morning. After yesterday's warm afternoon the water should still be cool and comfortable for trout this morning, but today's cooler air and a clear, cold night will chill it noticeably by Wednesday.

## Today

Tuesday is mostly sunny and much cooler, with a high of 51°, no rain and a north-northeast breeze gusting to about 15 mph. In this low, clear water, wait for the warmest hours of the early afternoon, keep low and back from the bank, and fish small nymphs slowly and deep through the pools at Ridgeview Park and in the catch-and-release water from Wayne Avenue to North Park. Mossy Creek's advice to carry a stream thermometer and fish the warmer hours fits a day like this.

## Tomorrow

Wednesday is cloudy, with a high of 48° after a clear night near 26°, and the rain holds off until late at night. The river should be unchanged and very clear but at its coldest of the week, so fish midday to mid-afternoon, slowly and deep; the cloud cover helps in clear water, and Mossy Creek notes that overcast days are when trout are most likely to cruise for small midges or mayflies.

## Weekend

Saturday, November 28, has some sun and a high of 58°, and Sunday, November 29, has intervals of sunshine and a high of 59°, with light rain of about 0.19 in arriving Sunday night. Coming after roughly 2 in of rain on Wednesday night and Thanksgiving, the river should be falling but still above its base flow and possibly stained on Saturday, and Sunday should be a little lower and clearer; check the gauge before going, since the size of the rise is uncertain.

## Next rain

The next meaningful rain starts late Wednesday night, November 25, about 0.52 in (90% chance), and continues as wind-driven rain on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, about 1.45 in more (99% chance), with north-northeast gusts near 33 mph; that is roughly 2 in in all, the largest rain in this log. The NWS forecast for WAYV2 ends at 7am Thursday with only the start of a rise, to 2.4 ft and 31 cfs, so it does not cover most of the rain; after the long dry spell some of it will soak in and the size of the rise is uncertain, but this much rain should bring the river up and color it for a day or more, and the cold rain will chill the water. Friday, November 27, is breezy with gusts near 29 mph, so Saturday, November 28, and the sunny, mild days from Monday, November 30, to Wednesday, December 2 (55° to 58°), look like the best chances as the water falls and clears.

## Water and rules

- Wayne Avenue to 2nd Street (North Park), about 2 miles: catch-and-release stocked trout water, year-round, no bait, single-hook artificial lures only.
- Ridgeview Park: put-and-take stocked water, 6 trout per day, 7-inch minimum.
- Basic Park: youth only, April 1 to June 15.
- Lyndhurst, upstream: about 3 miles of fly-fishing-only water on private land, stocked with fingerlings; free permit required.
- Grand Caverns to Grottoes: stocked trout water.
- Mercury advisory: eat no fish from the South River except trout.

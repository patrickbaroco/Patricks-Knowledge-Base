SIADH as the endpoint of the [[Hyponatremia|hyponatremia]] workup, and the hunt for its cause.

## Diagnosis — the two-step urine lock

- **Urine osm then urine sodium, read in sequence.** A high urine osm establishes ADH is *on* (excluding psychogenic polydipsia and beer potomania); a high urine sodium establishes aldosterone is *off* (excluding low-effective-volume states) — only their conjunction is *inappropriate* ADH. Interpret the urine sodium only off diuretics and without ATN. ([[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])
- **A serum uric acid under 4 supports SIADH.** The expanded intravascular volume raises the filtration rate and wastes urate, so a low serum uric acid (high [[Fractional Excretion of Uric Acid|FEUA]]) is confirmatory physiology, not a throwaway. ([[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])
- **It is one of five causes of inappropriate ADH** — with thiazides, hypothyroidism, [[Adrenal Insufficiency|adrenal insufficiency]], and [[Cerebral Salt Wasting|cerebral salt wasting]] — reached by excluding the other four (a normal cortisol and TSH, no thiazide, no intracranial salt-wasting). ([[RLR 003 - Hypertension and Severe Hyponatremia|RLR 003]], [[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])

## Secondary-cause hunt

- **SIADH is almost always secondary — hunt pain, medications, infection, and occult malignancy.** The classic infectious trigger is pneumonia (notably Legionella), the classic tumor a small-cell/neuroendocrine cancer of the lung — but **thoracic zoster is an even stronger SIADH trigger** and belongs on the schema. ([[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])

## Management

- **Confirm fluid restriction can work, then escalate.** Check that urine (Na + K) is less than serum sodium so restriction will succeed; restrict total fluid (not water alone) to ~1 L; add urea powder (which rebuilds the medullary gradient to excrete free water) in preference to salt tabs; reserve a loop diuretic to generate a hypotonic urine. Beware over-rapid correction and [[Osmotic Demyelination Syndrome|osmotic demyelination]]. ([[RLR 029 - Dyspnea and Disseminated Varicella-Zoster|RLR 029]])

#fishing 

## Best time to fish

Season runs November through late April, in three stretches: early season (Nov–Dec) has aggressive, hungry fish and higher water, with spawning in the Barrancoso River; mid-season (Jan–Feb) is prime top-water/sight-fishing time in warm, stable weather; late season (Mar–Apr) is prime-time for giants — cooler, fewer anglers, fish in peak condition after months of feeding. Pick the window based on whether you want action, trophy size, or solitude — the whole season is strong, just for different reasons, so no separate `peak_months` beyond the full season window.

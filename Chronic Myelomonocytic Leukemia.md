CMML — the disease behind a sustained monocytosis.

## What it is

Chronic myelomonocytic leukemia is a myelodysplastic/myeloproliferative overlap disorder defined by a sustained peripheral [[Monocytosis|monocytosis]]. It is graded by blast burden: **CMML-1 vs CMML-2** (higher blast fraction), with the boundary to [[Acute Myeloid Leukemia|AML]] at **20% blasts**. In the worked case a repeat smear revealed peripheral blasts and the marrow returned CMML-2. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])

## Reasoning points

- **Reach it from a sustained [[Monocytosis|monocytosis]] confirmed on smear** — with chronicity (prior counts) the strongest evidence, and flow cytometry to show a clonal population. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])
- **It can present through its complications** — [[Leukemia Cutis|leukemia cutis]], and immune cytopenias (a primary hematologic malignancy can drive AIHA/ITP, an [[Evans Syndrome|Evans]]-like picture). ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])
- **Prognosis is guarded** — treated with hydroxyurea for cytoreduction and hypomethylating agents (decitabine), with a median overall survival on the order of ~16 months, worse for CMML-2. ([[RLR 032 - Fever and Chronic Myelomonocytic Leukemia|RLR 032]])

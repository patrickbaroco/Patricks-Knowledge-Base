Three-way Foley irrigation that keeps the bladder clear of obstructing clots while native bleeding stops — a supportive measure, not a treatment of the underlying cause.

## What It Is
Continuous bladder irrigation (CBI) uses a **three-way (triple-lumen) Foley catheter**: one channel inflates the retention balloon, one continuously instills irrigant (normal saline), and one drains. A steady inflow-outflow flushes the bladder of blood before it can organize into clot ([[RLR 064 - Hematuria and Post-TAVR Perivalvular Leak|RLR 064]]).

## What It Does — and Does Not Do
- **It prevents clot/stasis obstruction.** By diluting and washing out blood, CBI stops clots from accumulating and causing **clot retention** (a painful, obstructed bladder) and catheter blockage.
- **It does NOT treat the underlying cause.** CBI buys time while **native bleeding stops on its own**; it exerts no effect on a tumor, a coagulopathy, or a vWF defect ([[RLR 064 - Hematuria and Post-TAVR Perivalvular Leak|RLR 064]]).
- **Its necessity marks severity, not therapy.** Needing CBI signals that bleeding is brisk enough to clot off the bladder — so if bleeding persists despite CBI and urologic source control, an unusual systemic contributor should be sought ([[RLR 064 - Hematuria and Post-TAVR Perivalvular Leak|RLR 064]]; see [[Bleeding Framework]] and [[Law of Proportionality]]).

## Practical Points
- **Titrate flow to effluent color** — faster with darker/bloodier output, slower as it clears; aim to keep the drainage light pink to clear.
- **Watch outputs vs inputs.** Irrigant volume must be subtracted from total drainage to gauge true urine output; a sudden drop in outflow suggests catheter obstruction requiring hand irrigation.
- **Complications** — clot retention if flow is inadequate, bladder distension/perforation if outflow is blocked, hyponatremia risk with hypotonic irrigants (saline is standard), and catheter-associated infection.

## Reasoning Note
CBI is a classic example of a temporizing measure mistaken for definitive therapy. Recognizing its limits keeps attention on **finding and controlling the source** of [[Hematuria|hematuria]] ([[RLR 064 - Hematuria and Post-TAVR Perivalvular Leak|RLR 064]]).
